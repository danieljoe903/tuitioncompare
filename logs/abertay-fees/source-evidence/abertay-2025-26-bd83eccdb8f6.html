Undergraduate Tuition Fees - International Students 2025/26

Show Hide

Undergraduate tuition fees for international students vary depending on your programme of study (also called an 'undergraduate degree course'). See table below.

Paying your deposit

You’re required to pay a deposit equal to 50% of your net tuition fee before you can apply for your Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies (CAS) – a mandatory document for your Student Visa application. The net tuition fee is calculated after deducting any University scholarships from the gross fee.

Following registration on your programme of study, the remaining balance of your net tuition fee will be payable in three equal instalments:

Students starting in September

  • A 50% deposit is due, with three equal instalments to be paid on 1 November, 1 January and 1 March.

Students starting in January

  • A 50% deposit is due, with three equal instalments to be paid on 1 March, 1 May and 1 July.

For example, if you’re enrolled on a course with a gross tuition fee of £15,000 and you receive an International Scholarship of £3,000, your net tuition fee becomes £12,000. Consequently, your deposit will be 50% of £12,000—that is, £6,000. The remaining balance will then be divided into three equal payments of £2,000 each.

Please note: In certain circumstances, if risk factors are identified in relation to visa applications, a student may be required to pay either the full first-year tuition fee or a higher deposit prior to the issuance of the CAS. Should this occur, you’ll be advised accordingly.

Fee guarantee

We offer all new international students a fee guarantee: the fee rate published for 2025/26 entrants will be the same each year and won’t increase during your course of study.

  • Funding for International students, including scholarships

Self-funding/sponsored students: how to pay tuition fees

Show Hide

Self-funding students: undergraduate

Students who need a student visa

If you’re self-funding your undergraduate degree, you’ll need to pay a 50% deposit before CAS and arrange payment of the remainder of your tuition fees as part of your online registration. Once you’ve completed online registration for your programme, the remaining 50% of your net tuition fee is payable in three equal instalments on the dates below: 

 September start

  • A 50% deposit is due, with three equal instalments to then be paid on 1 November, 1 January and 1 March.

Students who do not require a student visa

You can either pay fees in full or set up a monthly payment plan. You can set up a monthly payment plan during the online registration process.

For more information on your payment options, please read our Student Debt Management Policy. 

Sponsor Funding

If you are an overseas student requiring a student visa, and your sponsor is on the University’s approved list (see appendix B of the Student Debt Management Policy), your sponsor will be invoiced after registration.

In all other cases, the sponsor must pay your tuition fees in full before you can register. 

Paying Tuition Fees

  • Log onto the intranet – please do not share your log in details with anyone else, in accordance with University regulations.

  • Go to the MyFinance section.

  • Click on the option 'Pre-payment of Tuition fees (Overseas applicants only)'.

  • This will take you to our international payment system.

  • Full instructions on how to make your payment are provided on this system.

Remaining tuition fees and fees for all other student fee categories are made during online registration. Payment links will be provided during the registration process.
