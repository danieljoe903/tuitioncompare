                Are you eligible for a Scholarship?

                If you have any questions please contact admissions@london.aru.ac.uk.

                Undergraduate Scholarships

                Undergraduate Merit Scholarship

                Our Undergraduate Merit Scholarship is open to undergraduate students from the UK (and eligible EU/EEA students) who achieve 120 UCAS tariff points from A Levels, BTEC or equivalent qualifications.

                Who is it for? UK or eligible EU students studying a full-time undergraduate degree.

                How much is it worth? £1,000.

                How do I apply? You’ll automatically receive our Undergraduate Merit Scholarship if you meet the eligibility criteria and register as an ARUL student.

                Top Achievers

                Scholarships of £2,000 are available for the overall top achievers in Year 0, Year 1 and Year 2.

                Each year several scholarships are available to our top achievers throughout all courses and modules. The winners are presented with their scholarships during our Top Achievers event hosted three times a year. The scholarship is applied to your next academic year's tuition fees.

                Postgraduate Scholarships

                Masters Scholarship Programme

                ARU London provides several scholarships each year across our three postgraduate intakes. All applicants are offered the chance to receive a scholarship of up to 50% off their Master's Degree. To enter applicants must write 750 words or more about their previous degree, particular area(s) of interest within the field of business, and why they wish to study for a postgraduate degree.

                Please send all entries to uk@london.aru.ac.uk with the subject: ARU London MSP - <Application ID> - <Full Name>.

                Please ensure you have submitted your application for admission before sending your scholarship application.

                ARU Alumni Scholarship

                All alumni of ARU London will be offered a £2,000 scholarship if they wish to continue their studies at ARU London.

                Master’s Entry Scholarship

                Any applicant who has achieved a First Class honours degree from a university in the UK will be offered a £1,000 scholarship upon admission.

                Sports

                If you're a talented athlete, apply for our Anglia Ruskin Sports Scholarship worth £1,000.

                We take great pride in the fact that some of the most talented sportsmen and women in the country choose to study with us. We also understand how difficult it can be to balance the demands of competing at the highest level with a full academic life when you first arrive at university.

                We offer students competing at regional, national, or international levels, the opportunity to apply for a scholarship award worth up to £1,000 per year for three years. Each award is tailored towards the needs of the athlete and is delivered as a package of support that’ll add value to existing training programmes to help achieve both academic and sporting success.

                You can apply for a scholarship by contacting enquiry@london.aru.ac.uk or calling 0207 400 6784.

In addition, if you use our cookie tool to opt-out of certain cookies, your opt-out preferences will be remembered by placing a cookie on your device. It is therefore important that your browser is configured to accept cookies for your preferences to take effect. If you delete or clear your cookies, or if you change which web browser you are using, you will need to set your cookie preferences again.
