The BSc International Business (Top-Up) will help you develop the strategic, analytical, and professional skills needed to operate effectively in today’s global business environment. You will explore how organisations operate across different international contexts while developing an understanding of the cultural, ethical, and sustainability considerations that increasingly shape business decision-making and management practice.

The course combines academic theory with practical application, enabling you to critically evaluate contemporary international business challenges and develop evidence-based solutions to real-world issues. You will strengthen your ability to think strategically, communicate effectively across cultures, and engage confidently with diverse international stakeholders and business environments.

Throughout the course, you will also develop valuable research, problem-solving, and independent learning skills through undertaking an individual research project linked to your interests and career aspirations. In essence, the course will support you in developing the knowledge, confidence, and global outlook needed for employment, entrepreneurship, or further study within an international business context.

Careers

Get ready to navigate today’s globally-connected business world.

Gaining a full honours degree can further develop your knowledge and skills – and prepare you for the next step in your business career.

A degree in an international business can prepare you for a career overseeing a company's global operations, in which you could be responsible for a company's global business, international sales, or overseas manufacturing.

Typical roles may include: Export/Import Manager, Global Marketing Manager, International Business Consultant, Cross-Cultural Manager, or International Trade Specialist.

You might decide to stay at ARU and study for a Masters. There are plenty of options, including our MBA, MSc Marketing or MSc International Business. Take advantage of our Alumni Scholarship and get 20% off your fees.

We want you to be a step ahead when you graduate, so we offer:

  • courses designed with employers
  • tutors with professional industry knowledge
  • hands-on learning through Live Briefs, internships and placements
  • Ruskin Modules that help develop the skills employers value.

Alongside your course, there are lots of other opportunities to build your skills and experience:

  • add value to your CV with an ARU Certificate of Professional Development
  • get involved in a thriving volunteering programme
  • develop your brilliant business ideas
  • work with businesses to make a social impact.

We’re here to help you develop your CV and find that all-important role after graduation. Our Employability Service offers tailored career appointments, advice on writing your CV, help to complete job applications, and much more. You’ll have 24/7 access to an online Careers Centre too, which is packed with useful resources.

The support continues after you graduate, with lifelong access to our Alumni Career Centre.

Find out more about careers support at ARU.

Teaching and modules

Study over one year and develop the knowledge and skills that international business managers need.

You'll explore the complexities of operating across countries, and consider the shift towards more responsible business practice.

To complete the course, you'll work as part of a team to address a complex business issue.

Modules are subject to change and availability, and may vary by location. If you have the choice of optional modules, these are indicated with a *.

Module details

All assessment is designed to allow you to demonstrate what you’ve learned, and to make sure you’re developing the knowledge and skills you need to complete the course.

You can expect an interesting mix of coursework, essays, exams and activity-based assignments. Your coursework could include problem-solving activities, consultancy projects, presentations and group or individual reports.

You'll also be able to access support materials through our learning management system, Canvas.

Entry requirements

Requirements for Top-Up students

Entrants will normally be required to hold 120 Level 4 and 120 Level 5 credits (ie HND) from a relevant subject, or equivalent. Alternative qualifications may be accepted.

If English is not your first language you will be expected to demonstrate a certificated level of proficiency of at least IELTS 6.0 (Academic level) or equivalent English Language qualification, as recognised by Anglia Ruskin University.

Important additional notes

Our published entry requirements are a guide only and our decision will be based on your overall suitability for the course as well as whether you meet the minimum entry requirements.

Other equivalent qualifications may be accepted for entry to this course; please email [email protected] for further information. Applicants to undergraduate courses can also check our information about undergraduate entry qualifications.

You'll need a computer and reliable internet access to successfully engage with your course.

We don't accept AS level qualifications on their own for entry to our undergraduate degree courses. However for some degree courses a small number of tariff points from AS levels are accepted as long as they're combined with tariff points from A levels or other equivalent level 3 qualifications in other subjects.

Paying your tuition fees
If you're a UK student, you don't need the money to pay for university now.

If you accept an offer to study at university, you can apply for a Tuition Fee Loan to cover your course fees.

Both full-time and part-time students can apply for a Tuition Fee Loan. You'll start repaying the loan in the April after you graduate.

Living at university

We understand that affordability is an important issue when you’re thinking about studying at university.

The overall cost of university can vary depending on where you’re studying and which course you’re taking.

Costs may also be lower if you're living at home. However, some of the main costs you’ll want to think about include:

  • accommodation
  • bills
  • internet, mobile phone and calls
  • travel
  • food and drink
  • socialising and going out
  • study materials.

Most full-time UK students can apply for a Maintenance Loan to help cover living costs. More about maintenance loans.

We also have a range of offers to help with the cost of living.

Our Money Advice Service is on hand if you’d like to chat about living costs, budgets and managing your money. You can also find out more about student finance at one of our Open Days.

We offer a range of scholarships and bursaries for UK and international students, which provide extra financial support while you’re at university.

If you're eligible for a scholarship, you'll generally receive it automatically – there's no need to apply.

Our campus employment bureau, ARU Temps, can help you find part-time work that fits around your studies (including any time you may spend on placements). It's a great chance to enhance your CV and boost your income while you study.
