                Overview

                The Master of Public Health (MPH) at ARU London is designed to prepare students for careers focused on improving health and wellbeing at a population level. Centred around the key principles of health protection, health improvement, and service development, the course provides a thorough understanding of the social, economic, environmental, and political factors that influence health outcomes in diverse communities. London’s unique setting offers students a valuable opportunity to study public health issues within a live and varied urban environment.

                You will develop core skills in research, critical analysis, leadership, and communication while gaining experience in assessing health needs, planning interventions, and evaluating outcomes. The course explores real-world public health challenges using case studies and applied learning, and incorporates current thinking around sustainability, ethical practice, global citizenship, and inclusive care. With input from public health professionals and sector experts, the course content remains closely aligned with the needs of employers and evolving industry expectations.

                The programme includes a final major project where you will apply your learning to a real or simulated public health issue, allowing you to demonstrate your knowledge, practical ability, and strategic thinking. By the end of the course, you will be equipped to pursue a range of roles within local authorities, NHS and international organisations, NGOs, and public policy teams, or to continue your studies in a specialised or management-focused direction.

                Structure and content

                Over the course of your masters, you will study a total of 6 modules. See below for the modules which you will study:

                • Postgraduate Study Skills, Research Methods, and Ethics (Healthcare)
                  • This module supports your transition to postgraduate study by developing essential academic and research skills. You will learn how to design research projects, analyse healthcare data, and understand ethical principles in public health research.
                • Determinants of Population Health
                  • Explore the social, economic, environmental, and political factors that influence population health. This module focuses on understanding health inequalities and how public health professionals can address them.
                • Health Protection
                  • Learn how to assess and manage risks to public health, including communicable diseases, environmental hazards, and emergency planning. The module also covers surveillance systems and health risk communication.
                • Finance and Governance in Health and Social Care
                  • Examine how financial management, regulation, and governance structures impact health and social care delivery. You will gain insights into policy frameworks, accountability, and funding in public health systems.
                • Population Healthcare and Health Improvement
                  • Focus on the planning, delivery, and evaluation of health improvement programmes across communities. You will learn to apply evidence-based approaches to influence health behaviours and improve outcomes.
                • Postgraduate Major Project (Healthcare)
                  • Apply your knowledge to a substantial, independent project focused on a real-world public health issue. This capstone module allows you to design and complete research, evaluate findings, and demonstrate your critical and analytical abilities.

                These are delivered across three trimesters in full-time or part-time formats, with September, January, and May intakes available.

                This course uses a blend of classroom teaching, case studies, experiential learning, and practical assignments. Learning methods include:

                • Team-based and problem-solving exercises

                • Interactive lectures and debates

                • Guest sessions with public health professionals

                • Simulation activities and case study analysis

                • Capstone project grounded in real-life public health issues

                From the beginning, your learning will be linked to your career aspirations, with professional and academic guidance to help you chart your path in public health.

                                                                                • Oral presentations these offer the student the opportunity to develop and demonstrate ability to communicate orally, to different audiences

                                                                                • Examinations, in-class tests and online quizzes: these offer the student the opportunity to develop and demonstrate ability to work within strict time constraints and, in relation to the latter, to feedback on their ongoing learning

                                                                                Course Aims

                                                                                The course aims to enable master’s graduates to:

                                                                                1. Understand, respond to, and shape the varied, dynamic, and complex personal, community, and global perspectives that define population health.
                                                                                2. Manage practical public health challenges through the application of appropriate skills and theoretical knowledge.
                                                                                3. Think and act as a global citizen, with a focus on appropriate ethical approaches and futureproofing to deliver public health sustainability.
                                                                                4. Evaluate, synthesise, and utilise the varied information available to and for public health, and develop appropriate research frameworks for collecting and critically analysing public health information
                                                                                5. Appraise and evaluate theories for the management of resources to deliver public health and develop methods of sustaining and improving those resources.
                                                                                6. Understand, evaluate, and utilise appropriate theoretical frameworks related to public health interventions.
                                                                                7. Solve complex public health problems and make decisions through appropriate problem definition, research planning, analysis, and evaluation.
                                                                                8. Gain a thorough insight into contemporary research and leading-edge practice within the field of public health.
                                                                                9. Foster innovation, creativity, and generate originality and enterprise in approaching complex public health issues.
                                                                                10. Demonstrate self-direction and autonomy in research and scholarship and continue to advance knowledge and understanding, and master new skills to postgraduate level.

                                                                                Course Learning Outcomes

                                                                                Knowledge and Understanding

                                                                                Understanding and Application of Public Health Knowledge

                                                                                Graduates will critically evaluate the complex, dynamic nature of public health shaped by individual, societal, environmental, and global contexts, using contemporary theories and advanced scholarship to investigate and resolve public health challenges (A01, A02, A03).

                                                                                Research and Strategy Development

                                                                                They will synthesise diverse information sources to design population-appropriate strategies and interventions, while critically appraising research methods and applying them to contemporary public health issues (A04, A10).

                                                                                Technology, Communication, and Innovation

                                                                                Graduates will understand and evaluate the role of evolving technologies, including AI, in driving public health intelligence and innovation, and recognise the importance of entrepreneurship and creativity in advancing public health practice (A05, A09).

                                                                                Cultural, Ethical, and Global Perspectives

                                                                                They will assess the impact of DEIA and cultural differences, navigate cross-cultural challenges, and address globalisation and ethical dilemmas in the planning and delivery of public health interventions (A06, A07, A08).

                                                                                Intellectual (thinking), practical, affective and transferrable skills

                                                                                Critical Thinking, Communication, and Problem Solving

                                                                                Graduates will think critically and creatively, challenge assumptions, and communicate complex ideas clearly to diverse audiences. They will solve real-world public health problems through rigorous data synthesis, research, and application of theory to practice (B01, B02, B03, B04).

                                                                                Diversity, Collaboration, and Practice

                                                                                They will demonstrate awareness of DEIA principles in public health delivery, work collaboratively in interprofessional settings, and effectively manage group dynamics and tasks (B05, B06).

                                                                                Operational Competence and Professional Growth

                                                                                Graduates will manage risk, resources, and funding in public health projects, using information effectively. They will also engage in critical self-reflection, manage ethical dilemmas, and evaluate policy impacts while developing their professional practice (B07, B08, B09).

                                                                                Autonomy and Global Citizenship

                                                                                They will act with autonomy in learning and practice, apply knowledge to improve public health outcomes, and operate with a commitment to social justice, environmental stewardship, and global awareness (B10).
