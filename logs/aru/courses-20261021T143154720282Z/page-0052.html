FinTech and Data Analytics MBA

Master the latest advances in financial technology and prepare for an exciting career in a fast-moving area. Study at ARU in Chelmsford.

If you’re passionate about the possibilities generated by blockchain technology and digital currencies, this course is for you.

You’ll learn how to formulate data-informed trading strategies, financial models and business forecasts. You’ll create your own applications and analyse big data using R and Python, and make extensive use of our Bloomberg Financial Markets Lab.

At the same time, you’ll develop strategic, marketing and leadership skills to help you climb the career ladder.

Why ARU?

  • Learn how to create data-informed trading strategies, financial models and business forecasts

  • Gain skills and experience in strategy and leadership

  • Build your network with our industry contacts and guest speakers

  • Enhance your employability with our optional Professional Experience year

 

Study at an award-winning university

We were named Times Higher Education University of the Year 2023. We've also been awarded a Gold rating in the Teaching Excellence Framework – a reflection of the outstanding education we offer our students.

Careers

This course prepares you for an exciting career in an innovative, fast-moving area.

We teach leadership, management, strategy and marketing skills alongside technical skills, so you’ll graduate as a well-rounded candidate ready to ascend the career ladder.

Graduation doesn’t have to be the end of your time with us. You could choose to continue your academic career with our Business PhD – School of Management or Business PhD – School of Economics, Finance and Law. Take advantage of our Alumni Scholarship and save £400 on your fees.

Depending on your interests, as a FinTech and Data Analysis MBA graduate you could find yourself working for an online payment system, peer-to-peer lending or crowdfunding platform, online stockbroker, or robo-advisory service for individual investors, to name just a few examples.

You could also work in banking, insurance, or other financial services, or start up your own business. With our start-up hub and annual business competition, if you’ve got a great idea, you don’t even need to wait for graduation to make it happen.

ARU Business students are European Champions

A team of ARU students came first in Europe and sixth in the world in the prestigious Bloomberg Global Trading Challenge.

We want you to be a step ahead when you graduate, so we offer:

  • courses designed with employers
  • tutors with professional industry knowledge
  • hands-on learning which may include industry-led projects, placements or Professional Experience modules.

Alongside your course, there are lots of other opportunities to build your skills and experience:

  • add value to your CV with an ARU Certificate of Professional Development
  • get involved in a thriving volunteering programme
  • develop your brilliant business ideas
  • work with businesses to make a social impact.

We’re here to help you develop your CV and find that all-important role after graduation. Our Employability Service offers tailored career appointments, advice on writing your CV, help to complete job applications, and much more. You’ll have 24/7 access to an online Careers Centre too, which is packed with useful resources.

The support continues after you graduate, with lifelong access to our Alumni Career Centre.

Find out more about careers support at ARU.

Teaching and modules

Studying accounting and finance is about understanding the language of business. It equips you with the analytical skills to make sound financial decisions.

Laura Muncey, Deputy Head of School

Discover how FinTech is revolutionising how we do business, and prepare to become part of it.

You’ll gain hands-on experience using solving financial problems, generating predictive analytics, and practicing financial modelling and business forecasting, using tools and methods commonly used in the industry. You’ll also develop knowledge and skills in marketing, strategic analysis and formulation, and leadership and change management.

You’ll complete your MBA with a Postgraduate Major Project – a substantial research project focussed on a relevant topic of that interests you.

Modules are subject to change and availability, and may vary by location. If you have the choice of optional modules, these are indicated with a *.

  • Analytics Essentials: Transforming Business with Big Data
  • Financial Data Analysis Using Python
  • Principles of Marketing Management (30 credits)
  • Leadership and Change (15 credits)
  • Management and Strategy (15 credits)
  • Financial Data Analytics
  • Postgraduate Major Project

Module details

You can choose to study with us over two years (full-time), and take Professional Experience modules in Year 2, with a Preparation for Professional Experience module in Year 1.

Module details

Professional Experience modules are a great way to learn more about an industry. They take place in Year 2 of your course, with each module lasting approximately 12 weeks (or one trimester).

You can choose which modules to take. Options include project work on campus, and industry placements. Our Placements Team is here to help you secure a placement.

Assessment will take a variety of forms that reflect the innovative and varied nature of FinTech. These include written assignments, portfolio development, presentations, case studies, examinations and group work.

Assessment will be directed to both test your achievement in the outcomes of the modules but also to develop your wider skills and knowledge in the area of financial innovation.
