If you aspire to design innovative, sustainable spaces that contribute to a safer and more conscious world, this course is for you.

Studying in our School of Architecture and Planning, you’ll develop the creative, technical and professional skills to design buildings and places that respond to environmental and social challenges.

Prepare for the next stage of your architectural career, as you build the practical experience to achieve your Part 3 qualification and registration as an architect.

ARB Part 2 prescribed

Our Master of Architecture degree is prescribed (Part 2) by the Architects Registration Board. When you complete this course, you'll fulfil the second part of the three-part process to become a registered architect in the UK.

Why ARU?

  • Design innovative, sustainable architecture for a changing world

  • Experiment with technologies including 3D fabrication, computational design, robotics and extended reality

  • Learn from practising architects and industry specialists through weekly masterclasses

Study at an award-winning university

We were named Times Higher Education University of the Year 2023. We've also been awarded a Gold rating in the Teaching Excellence Framework – a reflection of the outstanding education we offer our students.

Careers

A thoughtfully curated programme of engaging lectures, tutorials and site visits, nurturing growth, critical thinking and my knowledge of architecture.

Susan, MArch Part 2 graduate

Advance your journey towards becoming a registered architect with ARU's Master of Architecture (MArch).

Accredited as ARB Part 2, this course prepares you for the final stage of professional qualification in the UK. You’ll graduate with the advanced design, technical and professional skills to progress towards Part 3 and a rewarding career in architectural practice, urban design and the wider built environment.

You could work in any architectural practice in the UK and abroad, create a start-up collective, work as a computational designer or product designer, work with planning authorities, or focus on interior and landscape design.

Graduation doesn't have to be the end of your time with us. You might decide to continue your academic career and join a research programme at ARU, such as our PhD Engineering and the Built Environment. Take advantage of our Alumni Scholarship and save £400 on your fees.

We want you to be a step ahead when you graduate, so we offer:

  • courses designed with employers
  • tutors with professional industry knowledge
  • hands-on learning which may include industry-led projects, placements or Professional Experience modules.

Alongside your course, there are lots of other opportunities to build your skills and experience:

  • add value to your CV with an ARU Certificate of Professional Development
  • get involved in a thriving volunteering programme
  • develop your brilliant business ideas
  • work with businesses to make a social impact.

We’re here to help you develop your CV and find that all-important role after graduation. Our Employability Service offers tailored career appointments, advice on writing your CV, help to complete job applications, and much more. You’ll have 24/7 access to an online Careers Centre too, which is packed with useful resources.

The support continues after you graduate, with lifelong access to our Alumni Career Centre.

Find out more about careers support at ARU.

Teaching and modules

Advance your architectural career by combining creative design, technological innovation, sustainability and professional expertise in a dynamic studio environment.

Marco Vanucci, Course Director

Develop the knowledge, creativity and professional skills to become a conscientious architectural practitioner.

Through studio-based learning, you’ll explore a range of technical and creative-practical topics while working with real clients, gaining hands-on experience and insights into every stage of a typical architectural project.

Alongside design practice, you'll examine issues shaping the profession, including sustainability, ethics and the role of architecture in society.

You'll complete the course with a substantial research project, investigating a topic that reflects your interests and career ambitions.

Module details

Assessment is progressive, allowing you to build on previously acquired knowledge and skills, with feedback and feedforward used to effectively support and facilitate such development. We provide tutorials, interim crits, final reviews, assessment literacy, and online resources designed to support you in attaining relevant skills to undertake the assessments.

Assessment methods include: design projects, portfolio assessment, coursework, case study reports, computer-based exercises, individual and group presentations, timed assignments, written dissertation and essays to promote an understanding of good practice is supporting claims with evidence and referencing, role-play tasks in all design modules’ live briefs to promote experience and reflective skills.

Facilities

You’ll be based in our School of Architecture and Planning, with use of:

  • model-making studios
  • industry-standard software for computer-aided design work
  • construction and surveying equipment
  • a fully equipped workshop with cutting-edge digital fabrication machinery and skilled staff
  • specialist 3D printers that are available to all students.

All our architecture students can take part in team activities such as designing and building structures on campus.

Being just 35 minutes from London by train allows for numerous short study and site visits and workshops.
