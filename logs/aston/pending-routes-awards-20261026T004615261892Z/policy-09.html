Course overview

The Master of Business Administration, delivered in the heart of Birmingham, covers core management disciplines, while building on Aston University’s strengths in business, technology, and enterprise. Founded by Birmingham’s employers, the University maintains strong industry connections, integrating these ties into its intense, experiential MBA learning experience that fosters an entrepreneurial mindset.

Our dynamic MBA is suitable for graduates of all disciplines, offering a platform to advance your career at a top business school. Core modules focus on strategic development, value creation, and business model transformation through business analysis, new and emerging technologies. You can specialise in Cybersecurity, Healthcare, AI and Digital Transformation, or choose the Business Management pathway, selecting options across specialities. Individual career planning and support prepare you for senior leadership roles.  The programme emphasises personal development. This comprehensive approach equips you with the skills and confidence to excel in all professional settings.

Course description

Master of Business Administration

A high-level programme for experienced managers who want to transform their careers to enable them to undertake leadership roles in our fast-changing technological and global environment.

What we will provide
An innovative and applied learning experience, emphasising holistic business challenges, active task-based learning and authentic assessments. With a focus on teamwork, leadership and real-world problem-solving, the programme provides a broad understanding of business topics and develops an entrepreneurial mindset, critical analysis and strategic thinking.

We are also an Adobe Creative Campus meaning you will have access to top-notch software licenses, including Adobe and Solidworks, commonly used in industry.

What you will provide
As an MBA student, adopting a growth mindset is crucial. Success hinges on applying yourself, embracing a passion for learning and cultivating the drive to excel. Adaptability, ambition and a commitment to continuous improvement are foundational traits for navigating the dynamic challenges of the business realm.

What skills we teach
This programme connects you to the future, it helps you drive change: change across society and change in your career.  Built on a tradition of working with business, technology and enterprise, it helps you leverage technology to enhance our tomorrow.

Career prospects
Aston University's MBA programme prepares participants for a variety of senior level careers in business, including management consulting, leadership positions in a range of disciplines, and entrepreneurship.

How you will learn

All participants within our MBA cohorts have extensive work experience, and our teaching methods are deployed to take advantage of these capabilities in order to enhance students’ high-level leadership and management skills and to develop them as global leaders. This is achieved via intensive learning opportunities, which are delivered through team work, real-life case studies, business challenges and simulations. The curriculum is organised into week-long sprints, separated by time for reflection, self-development and assignment preparation.

Interaction with guest speakers and access to networking events, alumni mentoring, internships, study visits and career fairs connect you with industry experts and potential employers, enriching your educational experience and preparing you for the next stage in your management leadership journey.

In the Aston Edge you will enhance your employability and global leadership through experiential learning and reflective practice. Employability support from our dedicated MBA Careers team, skill-building workshops, individual and group coaching will prepare you for both academic and professional success. Drawing on Aston Business School’s long track record in developing entrepreneurs and creating for small companies, all students take part in the Aston Startup Lab and undertake consultancy for real life clients.

Get the Aston Edge

Running throughout your MBA programme, the Aston Edge is a powerful and personalised tool to supercharge your career progression.

The Aston Edge

What you will learn

You’ll learn to lead all aspects of businesses and organisations. You’ll explore the societal and environmental contexts shaping global economies and develop strategies to address them. Importantly, you’ll gain self-awareness to become resilient, empathetic leaders and entrepreneurs capable of making tough decisions, managing change, and creating future strategies.

Our curriculum integrates mastery of key skills and concepts with their application in various settings. You’ll practice advanced decision-making across core management functions and specialise in your career focus areas. During the dissertation phase, you’ll complete projects that enhance your ability to apply technology in diverse contexts and make decisions in a global environment. Finally, you’ll undertake an individual research project to solve a business problem of your choice, demonstrating your ability to conduct independent research with a strong ethical, theoretical, and methodological foundation.

Course objectives

  1. Fosters Entrepreneurial Spirit: Leverages Aston University’s rich heritage in business, enterprise, and technology to cultivate an entrepreneurial mindset, empowering future business leaders to innovate and creatively tackle corporate and societal challenges.
  2. Elevates Professional Expertise: Enhances the existing experience of business professionals by developing advanced skills, knowledge, and behaviours essential for managing change and leading transformative initiatives in tomorrow’s organisations.
  3. Cultivates Leadership Excellence: Equips individuals with the collaborative and personal skills necessary to become effective, self-aware, inclusive and confident leaders, capable of steering diverse and complex global enterprises responsibly.
  4. Promotes Lifelong Learning: Encourages managers to reflect on their strengths and career development needs, fostering a culture of proactive, lifelong learning and continuous self-improvement.
  5. Drives Organisational Transformation: Provides participants with the tools and insights needed to transform themselves and their organisations, ensuring they are well-prepared to meet future challenges head-on.

Taught stage modules

For all pathways, in the taught stage you will study the following core modules:

Module Title  Credits
Strategy and Economics for Global Competitiveness
Knowledge of business and corporate strategies and the ability to apply them to analyse and develop competitiveness within regional, national and global markets are core elements in being future business leaders and aspiring entrepreneurs. This module intends to help you develop these skills and competencies. The delivery requires cognisance of the rapidly evolving global landscape and the VUCA (volatile, uncertain, complex and ambiguous) world in which competitive and resilient strategies need to be developed. Distinctive features of the module are the link between strategy and business economics incorporated in the module, the combination of learning and doing through the flipped classroom approach, case study analyses, formative presentations and assessment linked to in-depth company case studies.		 15
Financial Analysis to Drive Value
This module will equip you with the skills and competences necessary critically to analyse financial data, enabling you to make informed decisions and recommendations and to communicate these effectively.		 15
Strategic Marketing, Planning and Analytics 
This module develops your expertise within the world of strategic marketing. The module engages you with the development of the marketing concept and its importance within a real-world context. You will design and develop marketing plans within an experiential situation using appropriate marketing theories, frameworks and tools. You will learn how to conduct situational analyses, formulate suitable strategies and measure the results using appropriate metrics. You will develop and implement your marketing strategy and planning knowledge in an authentic situation which will support your future employability or entrepreneurial ambitions. 		 15
Operations Management Transformation
This module provides a contemporary account of good practice in operations management, covering critical organisational success factors whether in manufacturing or service, large or small organisations. Themes running throughout this module are organisational, integration and digitalisation, internationalisation and globalisation, and ethical sustainability management. You will learn theories, methods, and techniques relevant to these themes. Cases and guest lecturers are used to provide context. You will become equipped to become effective strategic operations managers by acquiring relevant skills and knowledge to improve responsible environmental social governance and enterprising behaviour.  Authentic assessment will be through formative group exercises (class presentations and debate) based on your experience and research. An individual summative exercise will use a practice-based simulation game with a reflective, analytical critique report).		 15
Leadership for Tomorrow
This module equips you to lead effectively in diverse and unpredictable environments, with a focus on responsible leadership, business resilience, equality, diversity, and inclusion (EDI). You will explore (un)ethical practices, ethical decision-making, and resilience strategies for disruption prevention. Through real-world case studies and experiential learning, you will develop skills in responsible leadership, managing change and crisis, and fostering sustainable practices aligned with ESG principles. The module emphasises leading diverse teams, promoting well-being, and driving responsible transformation. You will be prepared to lead with integrity, inspire resilience, and balance social, environmental, and organisational goals. This module is essential for future leaders ready to navigate a volatile, globalised world.		 15
Startup Lab Aston 
This module aims to equip you with the skills, knowledge and behaviours necessary to be enterprising/ to be creative, to engage with entrepreneurial activity in collaborative contexts, and to generate positive impact. By exploring the entrepreneurial mindset, delving into the stages of the entrepreneurial process, applying design thinking and sustainability principles, and fostering collaborative teamwork, you will develop the skills, knowledge and understanding necessary to think like an entrepreneur, explore entrepreneurial activity to generate solutions for your own and others’ businesses, innovate for a sustainable future, and collaborate for impact. 		 15

 Pathway B: Cybersecurity

Module title Credits
Entrepreneurial Strategies for Cybersecurity
Due to the interconnected nature of contemporary societies and their rapid digitisation, organisations are at continuous risk to their information systems and assets. This module seeks to provide you with a comprehensive knowledge of cyber risk management and skills to critically analyse and evaluate existing practices. 		 15
Transformational Leadership in Cybersecurity 
This module equips you with in-depth knowledge to manage a red team attack, and plan penetration testing activities. (Red teaming is a group of ethical hackers who act a potential attackers and simulate cyber attacks, exploiting security weaknesses). You will learn how to coordinate penetration testing while adhering to legal and ethical standards. Skills include managing intelligence gathering, supervising technical teams, and understanding aspects of psychology. The module fosters leadership, communication, problem-solving, and decision-making skills applicable to cyber security managerial roles.		 15

Pathway C: Healthcare

Module title Credits
Patient-Centred Care and Quality Improvement 
Healthcare systems work to meet the needs and expectations of populations, within resource constraints. Value-based healthcare is increasingly adopted as an approach to achieve this worldwide and aims to deliver outcomes that matter to patients at the lowest possible cost. The module examines the interventions that support its enactment, including the central role of measurement, process mapping, time-driven activity-based costing and the introduction of integrated practice units. Attention will also be afforded to implications for staff and the promise of value-based care in reconnecting healthcare providers to their core purpose. Through case studies, practical examples and exercises, you will develop the knowledge and skills necessary to lead and implement value-based change in healthcare organisations and systems. 		 15
People Management and Equality, Diversity, and Inclusion (EDI) in Healthcare 
It is increasingly accepted that care of patients requires care of healthcare providers. In the context of global shortages of healthcare workers, rising workforce dissatisfaction, burnout and moral injury, this module explores health labour market, systems and organisational dynamics and levers that provide opportunities to enhance employee experiences and outcomes. The evidence base relating to the healthcare workforce is fragmented. This module draws on and consolidates international evidence and insights from disciplines including human resources for health, management, and the professions (medical, nursing, AHP etc). It also recognises the contributions of the near fifty percent of the healthcare workforce who are in user, professional and system supporting roles. The module provides evidence-informed insights into factors supporting a sustainable healthcare workforce.		 15

Pathway D: Marketing

Module title Credits
Communications in a Digital Space
Marketing communications play a vital role for organisations, however the landscape in which they operate has become increasingly fragmented and dynamic and customers are playing a greater role in content creation, particularly via digital channels. It is important for you to understand the synergies between different communication tools in order to plan, implement and evaluate campaigns.		 15
Entrepreneurial Marketing and Innovation  
The module outlines the development of entrepreneurial marketing and its importance within a real-world context. You will develop and present a business plan using appropriate marketing theories, frameworks and tools. You will develop, implement and reflect upon your entrepreneurial planning knowledge and skills in an authentic situation which will support their future employability or entrepreneurial ambitions.		 15

Pathway E: AI and Digital Transformation 

Module title Credits
AI Revolution in Business and Society
This module provides you with an advanced and strategic understanding of artificial intelligence (AI) and its profound potential to reshape and innovate businesses and industries. Designed for future leaders, the course covers complex concepts in AI, machine learning, and automation, highlighting their distinctions and strategic business applications. It critically examines the theoretical frameworks, philosophical debates, and ethical dilemmas associated with AI, while focusing on innovative use cases and strategic opportunities for leveraging AI in various sectors. Through rigorous discussions, in-depth case studies, and engagement with real-world scenarios, you will gain the knowledge and skills needed to strategically implement and lead AI initiatives within your organisations.		 15
Navigating Digital Transformation – Innovating for the future  
This module will focus on the topic of digital transformation and explore how organisations make use of the opportunity and succeed in this new environment. You will then have flexibility to choose your own focus for your own assignment, applying these concepts to an area of your choice. This flexibility is built into the module to allow you to follow the study path which is most relevant to you.		 15

Programme outcomes

On successful completion of both the taught and dissertation stage, you will be able to:

  • Demonstrate a deep understanding of global management theories and practices, enabling them to apply this knowledge to complex real-world business challenges with confidence and insight.
  • Display comprehensive expertise across various management disciplines, equipping them with the skills needed to excel in diverse enterprise environments.
  • Practice the integration of management disciplines to inform strategic decision-making and change management, ensuring they can lead organisations and individuals towards sustainable success.
  • Exhibit enhanced analytical skills to interpret complex data and to think critically and creatively, empowering them to generate innovative solutions and visionary ideas that drive business growth and stakeholder value.
  • Demonstrate the ability to identify, evaluate, and capitalize on entrepreneurial opportunities, applying innovative thinking and strategic planning to launch and grow successful enterprises
  • Exhibit the capacity to provide well-informed opinions and engage in meaningful debates (oral and written) on best practice, contributing to the advancement of technological, digital and management practice to the benefit of institutions and society.
  • Display essential leadership skills that enable them to lead responsibly and effectively in diverse, dynamic, and multicultural business settings.

Any such increase shall always be in accordance with the law. When undertaking a placement year, a placement year fee applies.

Assessment

Assessment has been developed to reflect the innovative action-oriented and experiential learning which are essential to create the confident leaders of tomorrow.  For this reason there are no formal examinations, and we will assess whether you have met the programme learning outcomes via team assignments, individual reflection, portfolios and projects, as well as simulations, games, consultancy and real-life business challenges which have a technology and global focus.

Our assessments will challenge you to demonstrate your entrepreneurial, analytical, collaborative and leadership skills, as well as applying core academic models in real life business situations.

What is the ‘Aston Edge’?

The Aston Edge personal development programme is a unique, differentiating feature of the Aston MBA. Acting as a common thread running throughout the entire MBA course, this module focuses on the development of key leadership and personal skills highly sought after by employers. Whether you are looking to enhance your career, or to excel in a leadership role, the Aston Edge can help you take that next step.

The Aston Edge comprises a series of dynamic, engaging workshops delivered by key academic members of the university and high-profile external speakers. This will engage you in a powerful combination of cutting-edge leadership research and expert practitioner advice and experience. The new business world is global, dynamic, complex and fast moving. The Aston Edge workshop topics are carefully chosen to equip you with the skills to excel in this world at any level of business.

Our highly experienced career consultants will work with you in parallel with the MBA programme, to support your personal brand and career development plan, ensuring that the Aston Edge becomes a powerful and personalised tool to supercharge your career progression.

Whether you want to lead your own start-up, excel in your current organisation, or propel your career in a new direction, the Aston MBA will equip you with the personal and professional skills, knowledge and confidence to establish a rewarding and successful career path.

Our MBA is tailored to what you need, both personally and professionally. As well as elective modules to choose from, you can also tailor your Final Project to best suit your goals. We also work with you to develop your own personal development and career plan and you will gain a breadth of experiences including team coaching principles. This will really help you to progress over the duration of the MBA.

All this combined means that you will shape our MBA to best suit your personal and professional plans and we will help you to achieve your goals whilst you develop on the course.

What will my timetable look like?

We understand how important it is to know your timetable in advance for planning around work, childcare, or other responsibilities. Here's some basic guidance on what to expect regarding your student timetable.

Full-time programmes
As a rough estimate, full-time postgraduate students in the College of Business and Social Sciences should expect to spend about 12-15 hours per week in lectures and on-campus seminars. This is in addition to independent study, and there may be additional guided learning activities during certain weeks. The exact distribution of these hours will vary depending on your course and module choices.
Part-time programmes
Part-time students will have fewer weekly on-campus hours compared to full-time students. This reduction is proportional to the number of modules you're enrolled in.
Timetable availability
Complete timetables are usually released shortly before the start of your programme. This timing is necessary to ensure that we accommodate all course requirements and that rooms are allocated based on the number of students likely to enrol on each programme. We aim to provide these timetables as early as possible, to assist you in your planning.

As your induction week approaches, you will be sent more information about your timetable via email, and you will be able to access your timetable by logging into MyAstonPortal. For any further queries or information regarding your timetable, you can reach out to your programme director or the Student Support team.

Online/distance learning programmes
The above information also applies to students enrolled in online/distance learning programmes. Please note that both full-time and part-time distance learning students are still required to attend scheduled synchronous learning sessions.
