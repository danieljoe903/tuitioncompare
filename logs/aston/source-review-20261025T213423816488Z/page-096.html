How to pay your tuition fees

Please note that, if you accept our offer, for international postgraduate students 50% of your tuition fee is payable pre-enrolment.

Aston University London payment methods:

Bank Transfer

Please quote your student reference as outlined on your offer letter when making your payment to Aston University London.

Card payments

You can pay fees for your Aston University London course using our online payments platform: Convera GlobalPay for Students.

This fast and reliable service allows you to pay in your local currency by Visa/Mastercard, or by using a wide range of local online payment options including Alipay, UnionPay and more, without incurring any transaction charges. If card payments are not available as an option for your country, please pay our bank directly. The bank details are listed above.

Watch the "How to" VIDEO


MAKE A PAYMENT


Call our Aston University London Finance team on +44 808 175 0786 for any further assistance regarding making your payments.

Please be aware that we do not accept cash payment, any funds paid in cash over the counter at a bank will not be accepted. To reduce the risk of you being a victim of fraudulent activity, Aston University London will NOT ask you to pay any invoices into accounts other than the method stated above. Any change to this account will be an exceptional occurrence and any such request, seeming to be from Aston University London, should be subject to your own additional authentication or verification steps prior to making any payment(s). If in doubt, please check with your Aston University London representative by phone.

Payment of fees (tuition and accommodation): please note that due to greater global banking compliance requirements, all payments for fees, unless agreed otherwise by us in advance and in writing, should only be paid from personal bank accounts situated in your (or your parents/payee) country of residence or as approved by us. Payments made outside of this accepted practice may result in you not being accepted on (or being asking to leave) your chosen course and the fees being returned or (if held by a bank and therefore out of our control) having no fees returned by us.
