Course overview

Artificial Intelligence has already impacted people's lives day-to-day, but it also has a wider impact on businesses and their operations. In the fast-evolving world, businesses need to embrace AI tools and be ready for an AI driven revolution.

This course will allow you to understand an organisation’s business and digital transformation journey and critically evaluate the role of AI as an enabler of transformation. You will be exposed to AI and analytics focused tools, which together with essential leadership and change management capabilities, will equip you with the skills needed to lead organisation's business transformation processes.

Capgemini are a leading advisor and transformation partner to companies around the world, leveraging technology to enable business transformation for almost 60 years. They address the entire breadth of business needs, from strategy and design to managing operations and engineering, drawing on deep industry knowledge and technical expertise in cloud, data, artificial intelligence, connectivity, software, digital engineering, and platforms.​

Industry-driven learning

Capgemini will deliver guest lectures as part of the course, expanding your knowledge and experience of relevant topics. Additionally, you will have the opportunity to work on Capgemini defined research projects. You will also have the opportunity to undertake an internship with the company with access to Capgemini facilities - gaining hands-on practical experience. Capgemini will contribute to the design and delivery of one of the course modules.

This programme is co-developed with industry partners through Aston’s Centre of Excellence for Enterprise AI, ensuring you gain up-to-date, practical knowledge. You’ll also benefit from professional development sessions through the Aston Global Advantage, and have the opportunity to gain the ISACA Artificial Intelligence Fundamentals Certificate, validating your foundational expertise in AI.

Course description

AI for Business Transformation with Capgemini

The MSc AI for Business Transformation with Capgemini is designed to equip you with cutting-edge knowledge of AI technologies to make you highly employable in today’s evolving job market. This programme blends technological insight with strategic business acumen, preparing graduates for dynamic roles driving digital transformation (DT) across industries.

What we will provide
First-class teaching in the UK’s second-largest city, delivered by a diverse array of staff who will inspire and excite you. You will benefit from applied learning, research-informed teaching and constructive assessment. There are practical activities, including simulation exercises, reflective case studies, collaborative group work, thought-provoking class discussions as well as inspiring lectures and tutorials.

Through our partnership with Capgemini, you will receive guest lectures, have the opportunity to work on research projects and also complete hands-on internships guided and defined by Capgemini. The company will also contribute to the design and delivery of one of the course modules.

We are also an Adobe Creative Campus meaning you will have access to top-notch software licenses, including Adobe and Solidworks, commonly used in industry.

What you will provide
It is important you learn how to adapt a growth mindset to your studies – understanding that intelligence and success come from applying yourself, a willingness to learn and an ambition and drive to excel.

What skills we teach
Skills vary from those that relate to people, to others in processes and technology. You will acquire skills related to change management and leadership, project and process management, analytical thinking and problem solving, data analytics, business and IT alignment. The course is taught in partnership with Capgemini, meaning you will benefit from gaining skills from a global leader in AI and technology.

Career prospects
The World Economic Forum’s Future of Jobs report (2023) stated that AI and machine learning specialists together with data analytics and digital transformation specialists are the most prominent emerging roles. Graduates can pursue roles such as: Digital Transformation Consultant, Change Manager, AI Strategy Analyst, Technical Project Lead, Business Transformation Manager and Data-Driven Innovation Consultant.
 

How you will learn

The delivery will include large and small group activities, case studies and guest lectures from renowned practitioners in AI and Business/Digital Transformation, including from Capgemini.

Our online learning platform supports your studies with all the necessary digital resources, including lecture slides, access to e-books and online journals, and video recordings of teaching sessions. Additionally, your lecturers will be available for one-to-one support to help you with any challenging topics.

In the Aston Global Advantage module, you will gain a competitive edge by honing your professional skills and preparing for success in a fast-paced global environment. You will tackle real-world challenges, dive into entrepreneurship and work on team-based business simulations.

At the start of your course, you will be allocated a personal tutor who can offer individual study advice and guidance. You will also benefit from access to the University Library's extensive collection of over 25,000 books and electronic journals.

Gain the advantage

Our flagship professional development module provides a range of opportunities to apply your learning and increase your employability.

The Aston Global Advantage

What you will learn

You will gain an applied understanding of how generative AI and emerging technologies can shape business models, enhance performance, and support organisational culture change. Designed to address a market gap, this course offers a unique pathway for the future of AI in business.

The programme covers essential leadership and change management skills, project and process management, data analytics, and problem-solving techniques. You will also develop expertise in evaluating AI technologies, designing evidence-based transformation strategies, and applying the People-Processes-Technology (PPT) management framework to real-world business challenges. Through case studies, industry insights, and hands-on projects, you will emerge equipped to lead AI-enabled transformation initiatives responsibly, ethically, and with measurable impact.

Learn business, do business

Our classroom goes beyond traditional lectures, focusing instead on real-life projects and hands-on experiences.

Student experience

Course objectives

  1. Develop competencies essential for the understanding of the use of AI in a broad range of business contexts
  2. Equip you with a systematic understanding of how AI tools can support business digital transformation journeys
  3. Develop skills that help you to apply the people – processes – technology (PPT) management framework to achieve digital transformation and readiness to organisational changes
  4. Ensure you understand the importance of responsible and ethical use of AI and other emerging technologies
  5. Provide you with the opportunity to enhance your employability skills by equipping you with key professional skills and competencies required for success in a global business context, thus contributing to several UN sustainable Development Goals (SDG).

Taught stage modules

In the taught stage you will study the following core modules:

Module Title Credits
Leadership for Transformational Change
This module explores the dynamic interplay between leadership, change management, and organisational culture. It delves into the complexities of leading organisations through transformational change, emphasising the role of leadership in shaping, sustaining, and evolving organisational culture. The module provides theoretical insights, practical tools, and real-world case studies to equip you with the skills necessary to lead effectively in environments characterised by uncertainty, diversity, and constant change. Through a combination of lectures, discussions, and experiential learning activities, you will develop a deep understanding of how to foster a resilient and adaptive organisational culture in the face of ongoing change.		 15
Artificial Intelligence for Business
Rapid progress of information technology advances in business have intensified. New technologies built on the web3 infrastructure deliver far more complex technologies in easy instantly accessible ways. The complexity and impact of such new technologies on business is profound. Artificial Intelligence is testimony to radical changes businesses are grappling with. We are at the cusp of switching from wondering what AI can do for business and how to implement effective redesign of businesses. Currently, a pragmatic relearning of the core elements representing technology as organisational structures, business models and the reach of technology within these primary spheres is transformed, along with people (employees and customers). Hence, this AI module will be a timely primer towards these objectives.		 15
Predictive Analytics
This module introduces students to essential concepts of data analysis, including data types, visualisation techniques, data preprocessing, and dashboard design. Students explore statistical learning methods from a high-level perspective while gaining hands-on experience with real-world datasets. Through practical exercises and projects, learners develop critical skills for analysing and interpreting data, making data-driven decisions, and designing compelling visualisations. The module emphasizes employability by equipping students with in-demand analytical skills and encourages enterprise thinking through creative problem-solving. By engaging in experiential learning, students apply theory to practice, preparing for careers in data-driven industries. This module is key to building a solid foundation in data science and enhancing students' ability to communicate insights effectively.		 15
Digital and Business Transformation
This module explores the transformative impact of digital technologies on business models, driving changes in products, services, and organisational structures while automating processes. The widespread adoption of technologies such as the Internet, social media, data analytics, collaborative tools, AI, and cloud computing has revolutionised industries by removing traditional boundaries of time and geography. These innovations have fostered virtual communities of employees, customers, and suppliers, introducing new market demands. Understanding the mechanics and scale of this change is central to digital transformation, enabling organisations to harness these technologies to improve productivity, reduce costs, and stimulate innovation. This comprises the core content of the module.		 15
Responsible AI and the Governance of Emerging Technologies
This module develops your ability to critically evaluate AI regulatory and governance frameworks and assess the ethical risks associated with emerging digital technologies. You will apply advanced ethical frameworks to analyse organisational challenges, appraise the consequences of algorithmic bias for responsible managerial decision‑making, and formulate evidence‑based recommendations for the ethical use of AI. Drawing on contemporary research and real‑world case studies, you will build the judgement required to guide responsible and accountable technology adoption within organisations.		 15
Managing Projects
Project management knowledge has improved substantially over the period through collaborative contributions from industries and academia. However, there are still incidents affecting time, cost and quality of projects all across the industries and geographical locations. This module will enable you to understand characteristics of projects, applications of project management tools and techniques.		 15
Value Creation and Business Impact with AI
This module equips you with the skills to identify, design, and evaluate high‑value AI opportunities in organisational settings. You will analyse business processes to uncover inefficiencies, prioritise AI use cases, and develop AI‑enhanced business model concepts that clearly articulate value creation, data needs, and workflow integration. You will build ROI and KPI models to support evidence‑based evaluation for senior stakeholders, considering costs, benefits, and risks. Throughout the module, industry insights - especially from Capgemini - guide the refinement of proposals to ensure strategic alignment, feasibility, and real‑world applicability, culminating in a live practitioner‑engaged challenge.		 15
The Aston Global Advantage
The Aston Global Advantage is our professional development programme that runs throughout all MSc programmes at Aston Business School. Focusing on professional development and employability, this integrated module gives you the opportunity to identify, develop and apply the key transferrable skills you will gain throughout the year.		 15

Programme outcomes

On successful completion of both the taught and dissertation stage, you will be able to:

  • Critically evaluate AI technologies, data-driven methods, and digital transformation frameworks to determine appropriate AI-enabled solutions for complex business challenges.
  • Critically analyse organisational culture, stakeholder dynamics, and human factors to formulate evidence-based strategies for people-centred digital transformation.
  • Create AI-informed redesigns of business operations to produce measurable improvements in efficiency, decision quality, or service delivery.
  • Work effectively in teams to communicate, manage and deliver projects professionally.
  • Appraise ethical risks, governance requirements and societal implications linked to AI use within contemporary organisations.
  • Demonstrate advanced capability to guide organisational change that supports successful business transformation.
  • Create, interpret, and synthesise AI-generated and evidence-based insights to support senior decision-makers in strategic planning, operational improvement, and AI-enabled organisational transformation.
  • Undertake independent research by formulating well-defined research questions, critically evaluating and applying appropriate research techniques, and drawing informed conclusions and recommendations.

We may update our courses to reflect best practices and academic developments. If we do, we’ll let you know of any changes at the earliest opportunity by updating our course webpages. If you have already received an offer, we’ll write to you directly to let you know of the changes.

More information on fees

The United Kingdom government has confirmed that European Union (EU), other European Economic Area (EEA) and Swiss nationals commencing academic courses in England from August 2021 will no longer be charged the same tuition rates as UK students.

Tuition fees for students are reviewed annually and may increase in subsequent years in line with inflation linked to the Retail Price Index (RPI) to take account of the University’s increased costs of delivering the Programme. Why study AI for Business Transformation at Aston University?

Studying MSc AI for Business Transformation prepares you to lead AI-driven change where it matters most - within organisations. This industry-co-created programme, endorsed by Capgemini, uniquely approaches AI through a management and transformation lens rather than a purely technical one, aligning people, processes and technology to deliver real business impact.

You will develop future-ready skills in leadership, change management, analytics and AI strategy, grounded in ethical and responsible AI practice. Through applied learning, industry engagement and the opportunity to work on live business projects, the course equips you to meet the growing global demand for professionals who can translate AI innovation into sustainable organisational value.

What will my timetable look like?

We understand how important it is to know your timetable in advance for planning around work, childcare, or other responsibilities. Here's some basic guidance on what to expect regarding your student timetable.

Full-time programmes
As a rough estimate, full-time postgraduate students in the College of Business and Social Sciences should expect to spend about 12-15 hours per week in lectures and on-campus seminars. This is in addition to independent study, and there may be additional guided learning activities during certain weeks. The exact distribution of these hours will vary depending on your course and module choices.
Part-time programmes
Part-time students will have fewer weekly on-campus hours compared to full-time students. This reduction is proportional to the number of modules you're enrolled in.
Timetable availability
Complete timetables are usually released shortly before the start of your programme. This timing is necessary to ensure that we accommodate all course requirements and that rooms are allocated based on the number of students likely to enrol on each programme. We aim to provide these timetables as early as possible, to assist you in your planning.

As your induction week approaches, you will be sent more information about your timetable via email, and you will be able to access your timetable by logging into MyAstonPortal. For any further queries or information regarding your timetable, you can reach out to your programme director or the Student Support team.

Online/distance learning programmes
The above information also applies to students enrolled in online/distance learning programmes. Please note that both full-time and part-time distance learning students are still required to attend scheduled synchronous learning sessions.
