Course overview

Please note from April 2026 MSc Global Business is changing names to MSc Global Business and Management. Programme details, entry requirements and fees will remain the same.

Study in-person at Aston University London in one of the world’s most exciting cities. Developed to deliver interdisciplinary learning and flexibility, this course enables you to tailor your studies to suit your own requirements and interests.

This programme will provide in-depth insights and understanding of the contemporary business landscape, tailored for both recent graduates and working professionals from local, national, and international backgrounds.

Throughout this course, you will delve into cross-border operations, examine various business functions and their role in international commerce alongside exploring strategic management and economics in an international business context. You will gain versatile skills to pursue diverse career paths and excel in global management roles.

On this programme you will learn to collaborate and flourish through project-based learning with students in other digital technology subject areas. This approach of learning mimics the real-life working world, where your career prospects will be enriched by working alongside people with different skill sets, viewpoints, and approaches.

The location of your learning offers unparalleled opportunities for networking and industry connections, thanks to its prime position in the heart of the capital.

Course description

MSc Global Business and Management (London)

Our MSc Global Business and Management course aims to equip you with the theoretical knowledge and practical skills needed to thrive in a variety of roles and business functions globally. By being based in London you will be part of a vibrant cosmopolitan city and community, renowned for its diverse skills, cultures, beliefs, and wealth of knowledge.

What we will provide
First-class teaching, in the UK's capital city, delivered by a diverse array of staff who will inspire and excite you. You will benefit from applied learning, research-informed teaching and constructive assessment. MSc Global Business and Management will give you the ability to think strategically and deliver successful international business strategies. 

We are also an Adobe Creative Campus meaning you will have access to top-notch software licenses, including Adobe and Solidworks, commonly used in industry.

Aston University London is a gateway to a multitude of industry connections and networking opportunities, set against the backdrop of a city known for its international diversity and cultural richness.

What you will provide
It is important you learn how to adapt a growth mindset to your studies – understanding that intelligence and success come from applying yourself, a willingness to learn and an ambition and drive to excel. It is important to engage in self-directed learning and take full advantage of the knowledge and resources available throughout your degree.

What skills we teach
Through lectures, collaborative group discussions and applied learning, you will develop key skills sought after by employers, including teamwork, communication and leadership. You will develop critical analysis, strategic thinking and problem-solving abilities applicable across diverse situations. You will gain a knack for applying theory to real-world scenarios, deepening world understanding.

Career prospects
As an Aston University Global Business and Management graduate, you will be equipped for a range of different career options. Graduates have been successful in finding employment in roles such as management consultant, research analyst and strategy consultant, working for companies like HSBC and KPMG.

By studying in London you will have prime location to open up numerous employment and career opportunities, thanks to the city's extensive industry connections and vibrant professional network.

How you will learn

At Aston University London, every programme on your chosen pathway will begin with a common landing module to introduce key fundamental concepts required by the rest of the pathway and to introduce you to the learning and teaching style of our innovative London courses. 

You will be taught by a combination of academic and industry experts to ensure you have the best possible chance of success in your degree. Our block teaching model means you will be able to plan your time around two days on campus each week, with the opportunity to spend more flexible time working on your individual and group projects. You will study at most two modules at a time, allowing you to focus in-depth on your chosen specialism and continually work on projects.

What you will learn

Our programme blends specialised knowledge with practical skills, covering the fundamentals of international business. Exploring diverse theories and concepts, the course unpacks the dynamics of international trade, examines how companies structure their overseas operations and evaluates the influence of foreign direct investment on host countries.

In the taught stage, you will delve into advanced international business topics. You will analyse various international business strategies and compare business dynamics in developing economies to more economically developed countries. You will also explore factors influencing international business strategies, such as economic, management and policy issues, developing your ability to think strategically and deliver successful international business strategy. The programme also emphasises transferable skills like teamwork, leadership and communication, equipping you for a versatile career in international business.

In the dissertation phase, you will undertake a research project on an international business topic of your choice, showcasing your ability to conduct independent research with a clear consideration of ethics, theory and methods.

You will also benefit greatly from the location of your studies, with around one-third of all international students in the UK residing in London, Aston University London provides a dynamic, multicultural learning environment that enhances both personal and professional growth.

Course objectives

  1. Meets the needs of local, national and international candidates, enabling them to learn by the exchange of experience as well as formal academic study.

  2. Develops the lifelong learning and personal development skills that equip you to aspire to senior positions and enhance your employability.

  3. Enables you to reflect upon and develop your own position in relation to corporate responsibility, sustainability and ethics.

  4. Develops awareness of skills needed to work effectively in cross-cultural and interdisciplinary teams.

  5. Develops an understanding of the global business environment (including shifting competitive advantages) that influence business decisions and operations.

  6. Develops an ability to assess the effects of major economic, policy, social and technical trends and factors on businesses operating internationally.

  7. Develops the ability to assess the challenges and opportunities involved in the management and operations of multinational enterprises and other enterprises operating in international contexts.

  8. Develops inquiry, applied research and problem-solving skills to enable lifelong learning and professional development.

Enterprise Strategy (BMM108L)
This module equips you with the analytical and practical skills to evaluate business opportunities and develop an understanding of what it means to be entrepreneurial in different contexts. You will explore entrepreneurial action and challenges in relation to commercial start-ups, social enterprises, family businesses, and corporate venturing. Entrepreneurial skills will be developed through in-class activities and case studies, supported by guest lectures from established entrepreneurs. The module encourages you to consider entrepreneurship as a viable alternative to traditional employment and to develop an entrepreneurial mindset.		15
International Marketing Management (BMM115L)
This module applies key marketing principles and concepts in an international context. You will develop your decision-making skills in the formulation and implementation of international marketing plans, while also building cultural sensitivity alongside your analytical and creative abilities.		15
Simulation for Management Decision Making (BBM115L)
This module focuses on the modelling of business systems using computer simulation. It provides you with the knowledge and skills required to critically analyse complex problem situations and develop solutions through the use of simulation models.		15
Effective Management Consultancy (BBM104L)
This module introduces you to different approaches for structuring consulting interventions and helps you develop an understanding of both effective and ineffective consulting practice. You will be better prepared to carry out successful consulting projects within organisations, whether as an external consultant or as a line manager addressing a one-off problem-solving task. Working in teams, you will apply team coaching principles to a self-selected challenge and use Soft Systems Methodology (SSM) to develop your consulting, analytical, and problem-solving skills.		15
International Economic Environment (BBM117L)
This module equips you with the ability to evaluate and analyse real-world economic issues that impact global business operations. By critically examining the international macroeconomic environment, you will gain insight into how these factors influence strategic decision-making and market outcomes for firms operating in an international context. You will also learn to synthesise the various factors shaping market behaviour and draw international comparisons, considering their implications for business performance and consumer welfare.		15
International Business Strategy (BBM107L)
This module explores the strategic challenges and opportunities that firms face in a globalised economy. You will examine key factors influencing international business decisions, including market entry strategies, competitive advantage across regions, and the impact of political, economic, and cultural environments. You will also study strategic alliances, mergers and acquisitions, and the management of multinational enterprises. By integrating theory with real-world case studies, you will develop the skills to formulate and implement international strategies, assess global risks, and adapt to the complexities of operating across borders in a dynamic business environment.		15
Innovation in the UK and Global Business Environment (BBM129L)
This module provides you with an in-depth understanding of how digital technologies are reshaping industries, driving innovation, and transforming business models, alongside the practical skills and strategic insight required to lead and implement effective digital transformation initiatives in a dynamic business environment.		15
Aston Global Advantage (BSM100L)
The AGA will allow you to reflect on the benefits of working in a multicultural environment, harnessing the skills associated with intercultural communication. This module focusses on personal and professional development harnessing collaboration from stakeholders from a range of communities and sectors. The module prepares you to be informed practitioners and where applicable they will be considering and evaluating research from a wide variety of sources throughout the module. This module does not require any research for which ethical approval will be necessary.		15

Programme outcomes

On successful completion of both the taught and dissertation stage, you will be able to:

  • Promote social responsibility, sustainability, accountability and ethical practice in business practice.
  • Critically evaluate a range of complex situations, which may include finance, accounting, organisational behaviour, strategy, IT security, analytics, artificial intelligence, taking account of the inter-relationships of these topics and their interactions with other areas of business, industry and the external environment.
  • Derive a range of transferable skills that are necessary for a successful career in business and enhance your employability. These may include: IT and computing skills, presentation and report writing skills, communication and team working (collaboration) skills, self-confidence, reflective practice, problem solving, leadership, decision making skills and ability to assimilate into multicultural and cross-cultural work teams.
  • Derive the ability to undertake independent research with an applied business focus.
  • Critically evaluate and construct knowledge of the changing global business landscape and developments and innovations in business practice.
  • Critique and apply core theories in international business.
  • Critically analyse and evaluate international business theories.
  • Evaluate knowledge and derive skills required for assessing and developing international business strategies and operations.
  • Critically analyse and evaluate the knowledge and skills required to understand and assess the international business environment.

Entry requirements

One of the following:

  • 2:2 (lower second-class honours) degree or equivalent
  • Equivalent overseas degree recognised by Aston University (see international students tab below for more information)
  • Additional requirements
  • International students
  • Extensive work experience

You will also need to provide:

  • A professional reference
    • If you are a current student or recent graduate (within the last 2-3 years), we will ask for an academic reference
    • If you are currently employed and not a recent graduate, we will ask for a professional reference
  • An official academic transcript of your university grades to date
  • A completed application form.

More information on fees

The United Kingdom government has confirmed that European Union (EU), other European Economic Area (EEA) and Swiss nationals commencing academic courses in England from August 2021 will no longer be charged the same tuition rates as UK students.
