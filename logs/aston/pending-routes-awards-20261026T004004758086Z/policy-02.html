Funding and scholarships

Finance

We believe money shouldn't stop you from pursuing your academic ambitions

Whether you're moving away from home for the first time, or returning to full-time education, student finances can seem a little daunting. Here are some simple steps to help you stay on top of the pennies. 

Paying Your Deposit

All International students and Home postgraduate students will be required to pay a deposit to secure a place on their chosen course. The exceptions are:

  • Undergraduate and postgraduate students who take out a student loan
  • Government-sponsored students

Your deposit will then be deducted from your first-year tuition fees. Details of how and when to pay your deposit will be outlined in your offer letter. If you have any questions, please contact the Admissions team.

Funding Your Studies

How you can fund your studies:

  • Student loan
  • Receiving bursaries and scholarships from Aston
  • Parental contributions
  • Working part-time

Loans

There are two types of student loan to help you cover the costs of university. You pay these loans back after you finish your course, when you’re earning over £25,000 per year.

Tuition fee loan: available to undergraduate students from the UK, tuition fee loans are paid directly to your university or college by the Government. You can expect to be paid any amount up to the maximum, which equals the cost of fees per year.

Maintenance loan: available to full-time undergraduate students from the UK, maintenance loans help pay for living costs such as food, travel, rent, course materials and accommodation. The amount you are awarded is dependent on household income. The maximum loan you can receive is £10,277 per year.

For more information regarding applying for Student Finance England as a Home student, please visit: Home Students | Aston University 

Support

It's important to us that your time at University is as enjoyable, productive and worry-free as possible, so there are plenty of places at Aston for advice on money matters.

Our friendly and experienced Student Advisor's in the Aston University Student Advice Team offer information on a number of different funds, trusts and bursaries you can apply to for financial support. In the Students' Union's Student Advice Centre offers extensive independent information, advice and representation services. Both of  these team's expertise covers a wide range of areas, including tuition fees and financial worries.

Our hardship fund is called  the Aston Student Support Fund

It’s reassuring to know that if finances get tricky during your studies, Aston University offers hardship funds that can help you get back on track. Find out more here. 

Scholarships for 2026/27 entry

We offer a range of scholarships to ensure that anyone who is qualified to come to Aston University can do so, regardless of their financial circumstances. We are committed to supporting the most talented and hardworking students to achieve their potential through schemes that help lower tuition and living costs.

Students do not need to apply to be considered for a scholarship because Aston University will use UCAS data, household income information provided by the Student Loans Company and information held on University records for placement years, to determine awards. Scholarship entitlement will normally be determined by the 30th November 2026. Students who are eligible for one or more of the Aston Scholarships will receive communications concerning their award/s through My Aston Portal (MAP).


Are you an international student?

  • We also offer a range of scholarships designed exclusively to support you.

Scholarships for UK students are listed below: 

Aston Support Scholarship

Successful applicants will be awarded £1,250 every year of their degree, totalling £5,000.

Available to UK students classed as care leavers, young carers and estranged students, children from a military family or refugees.

No application necessary. The University will automatically assess your eligibility using information on your A Level (or other) results provided by UCAS or at enrolment. The number of Aston Support Scholarships is not limited. 

If you have been selected to receive this scholarship, we will contact you.

Aston Forward Scholarships

Forward Scholarships are funded by alumni and friends of Aston. As of 2026/7, they are worth a total of £5000 across your degree (or £8000 for Medical students), with £2000 awarded to all recipients in the first year and £1500 in the second and final years (and in each year of study for Medical students). 

Available to UK students with a household income of less than £25,000, these scholarships will not be awarded on financial need or academic achievements alone. The scholarships will be awarded to students with amazing ambitions; students wanting to create a better world through community activity or business ideas, or to those who have overcome significant barriers to study at Aston. 

It is necessary to apply for Forward Scholarships. You will be invited to apply once you have completed the pre-enrolment process on My Aston Portal. Application dates for 2026 are 19th August to 5th October. Any applications received after this date will not be considered. A limited number of Aston Forward Scholarships are available.

Aston University must receive your household income assessment, carried out by the Student Loans Company (SLC). We strongly advise you to start this process now. You do not need to take a loan to prove your household income, information as how to do this can be found here

View full details on the Aston Forward Scholarships page.

Aston Placement Scholarship

Successful applicants will be awarded £1,250 to support living expenses during their placement year.

In order to receive the Placement scholarship, you must have an Approved placement record on MAP at the time of payment, which will be the end of November 2026. Details on how to get your placement approved, and the Placement guidance pages can be found here Placement Year: Essential Information | Aston University

Available to UK students with a household income of £42,875 or below, those taking an unpaid placement year, or those undertaking a placement abroad.

No application necessary. If you are selected to receive the scholarship, the university will let you know at the start of your placement year or once your placement details have been finalised - you will need to have registered your placement activity for Term 1 by 1st November 2026.

For students where the assessment is based on household income, the university will normally make an assessment in year one.

Aston Aspirational Scholarship

Available to UK students with a household income of up to and including £42,875.

Successful applicants will be awarded £500 in year one of their study.

No application necessary. The University will automatically assess your eligibility using information provided at enrolment. The number of Aston Aspiration scholarships is not limited.

If you have been selected to receive this scholarship, we will contact you shortly after your place at Aston has been confirmed.

The Ernest Edward Scholarships for Asylum Seekers

Aston University is delighted to offer up to four sanctuary scholarships for students who are seeking refuge in the UK. These scholarships will benefit asylum seekers or those who have been granted a form of limited permission to stay in the UK, following an unsuccessful asylum application, and who are therefore prevented from accessing student finance.

Find out more and apply.

MSc Motorsport Scholarship Programme 

Aston University is excited to introduce the MSc Motorsport Scholarship Programme, designed exclusively for home students. This initiative presents an exciting chance for aspiring minds to pursue a master's degree in MSc Future Vehicle Technologies.

Through this pioneering opportunity, we aim to support and uplift talented individuals from underrepresented backgrounds, promoting diversity and excellence within the dynamic automotive industry.

Frequently Asked Questions

If I'm eligible for a scholarship, how will I receive my scholarship payments?

Undergraduate Home students receive all scholarship payments through the Aston Journey Portal (excluding tuition fee discounts). Once funds have been released, you will receive an email to your Aston email address confirming that your scholarship funds are available to be withdrawn.

How do I access the Aston Journey Portal?

You can log on to the Aston Journey Portal via www.astonjourney.co.uk. 

I don't have log-in details for the Aston Journey Portal?

If you have not previously received a payment via the Aston Journey Portal, your account has not yet been created. Your account will be created when your first payment is released, and you will receive an email to your Aston email address with your account log-in details. You are encouraged to change the original password that you will receive to one you that you are more likely to remember.

How do I change my Aston Journey password?

Once you have an Aston Journey account, you can reset your password here.

Once the funds have been released to my Aston Journey account, how do I withdraw the funds?

Log on to Aston Journey and watch a short video of the withdrawal process here.

When do I need to withdraw my funds by?

You have until 31st July to withdraw any available funds. After this date, funds will be removed from your Aston Journey account. Once removed, funds cannot be reinstated. 

I am struggling to access the Aston Journey Portal/withdraw my funds, who can I contact?

If you are having trouble accessing your funds, please contact Aston Journey directly. Their email address is: Student.Info@AstonJourney.co.uk. If you have a query about the scholarship itself or the award, please contact ug_scholarships@aston.ac.uk.
