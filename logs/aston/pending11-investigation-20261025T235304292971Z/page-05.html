Computer Science

Computer systems are an integral part of our day-to-day lives, and online security is now a critical and high-profile business issue. The need for software developers, designers and programmers has never been greater, and there is unprecedented demand for cyber expertise to protect our economy and infrastructure.

Our degrees use creative and interactive approaches to provide immersive learning experiences. You’ll develop specialisms to suit your interests and goals, learn programming skills across multiple languages and platforms, and understand the legal, social and ethical contexts of being a responsible computer practitioner.

These dynamic and varied programmes will equip you with the skills needed to keep pace and contribute to this exciting and rapidly evolving field.
