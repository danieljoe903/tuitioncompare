BA (Hons) Painting, Drawing & Printmaking at Arts University Plymouth offers the only undergraduate degree in the UK that brings these three disciplines together in an holistic way, building on their rich history of nourishing and challenging each other.

<p>On this course, you'll explore <strong>painting</strong>, <strong>drawing</strong> and <strong>printmaking</strong> as three interconnected ways of thinking and making. You'll be given the space, support and practical skills to discover your artistic voice through imaginative questioning, material experimentation and skilled invention.<br /></p>

Find out more about BA (Hons) Painting, Drawing & Printmaking at Arts University Plymouth.

What you’ll learn and how you’ll study

<p>You’ll learn through <strong>hands-on experimentation</strong>, <strong>testing materials</strong>, <strong>processes</strong>, <strong>approaches</strong> and <strong>ideas</strong>, and reflecting on what you’re doing and why. Regular tutorials, seminars, lectures and group discussions help you develop confidence, identify relevant contexts for your work, and engage with diverse histories and current debates. </p> <p>Workshops introduce traditional techniques, including intaglio printing and making your own paints from raw pigments, which you’ll explore alongside technologies and know-how from across Arts University Plymouth’s world-class facilities.</p>

A student using the facilities in our fine print studio.

<p dir="ltr">You’ll work in a specialist studio environment designed to support focus, curiosity and ambition. As well as course teaching, you can book additional access to specialist facilities for self-directed development through SmartHub, with a guaranteed 11 hours each week during the teaching year.</p> <p>In printmaking, you can develop a broad technical range, with access to processes such as screen-printing, etching, drypoint, aquatint, lino, woodblock, letterpress, monoprint and more, supported by professional presses and equipment. </p> <p>Arts University Plymouth was ranked <strong><a href="https://www.aup.ac.uk/posts/arts-university-plymouth-whatuni-student-choice-awards-2026" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">#1 in the UK for Facilities</a></strong> (Whatuni Student Choice Awards 2026).</p>

Trips, exhibitions and the contemporary art world

<p>You’ll learn first-hand from artworks through visits to exhibitions in the South West, London and cities such as Berlin and Barcelona, helping you consider your place in the wider landscape of contemporary art. </p> <p>Our links with cultural and community organisations in and around Plymouth offer opportunities throughout the course to explore the roles of an artist and to exhibit your own work.</p>

'Distant' – Student work by Madison Allen, BA (Hons) Painting, Drawing & Printmaking graduate and 2025 Brownston Fresh Talent Award winner

Careers and future opportunities

<p><strong>Career opportunities include:</strong> Exhibiting Artists, Teachers, Lecturers, Art Therapists, Illustrators, Curators, Producers, Gallery Managers, Arts Administrators, Community Arts Organisers and Creative Entrepreneurs. </p> <p>Many of our award-winning students continue to postgraduate study at Arts University Plymouth and other institutions nationally, such as The Royal Drawing School and The Royal College of Art. </p> <p>You’ll also be supported by a dedicated Careers and Enterprise service, accredited by AGCAS (Association of Graduate Careers Advisory Services).</p>

Postgraduate progression with the Royal College of Art

<p>Graduates of this course can access application guidance, portfolio preparation and progression planning for relevant taught postgraduate programmes at the Royal College of Art through our progression agreement.</p> <p>Your undergraduate degree is taught and awarded by Arts University Plymouth. Applications to the RCA follow its normal admissions process, and applicants must meet the entry requirements for their chosen programme.</p> <p><a href="https://www.aup.ac.uk/study/undergraduate/royal-college-of-art-progression">Explore the RCA progression route and scholarships for 2027/28 entry</a>.</p>

Integrated Foundation Year

<p>This degree is also available as a four-year <a href="https://www.aup.ac.uk/courses/undergraduate/integrated-foundation">Integrated Foundation</a> option. Our <a href="https://www.aup.ac.uk/courses/undergraduate/integrated-foundation">Integrated Foundation</a> provides you with a preparatory year that will support your creative development, through a broad understanding of art, design and media skills relevant to your chosen degree subject. Learning through experimentation, exploration and discovery will support you in progressing to the next level of your degree at Arts University Plymouth.</p>

What are my options after completing this degree?

<p>After graduating from BA (Hons) Film &amp; Screen Arts, you could continue your studies at Arts University Plymouth on one of the postgraduate courses listed below. Our graduates are entitled to a 15% discount on tuition fees for our MA programmes.</p> <p>Through our <a href="https://www.aup.ac.uk/study/undergraduate/royal-college-of-art-progression">partnership with the Royal College of Art</a>, graduates of this course can also access application guidance and portfolio preparation for relevant RCA postgraduate programmes.</p>

MA Fine Art
Deepen your studio practice, within critical and contemporary contexts, develop your ideas and work in a practice-led, interdisciplinary environment.
MA Design (Communication)
Explore how current social, critical and technological paradigms are changing how designers create and distribute narrative-driven artefacts and campaigns.
MA Design
Develop products, practices and applications for contemporary society and imagine design beyond the status quo of production and consumption.
MA Museum Studies
This postgraduate course offers a unique opportunity to reimagine the role of the museum within contemporary socio-political debates

Frequently asked questions

What makes BA (Hons) Painting, Drawing & Printmaking at Arts University Plymouth unique?
<p>Arts University Plymouth offers the only undergraduate degree in the UK that brings painting, drawing and printmaking together in a holistic way, taught through imaginative questioning and skilled invention across the three interconnected disciplines.</p>
What will I study on this course?
<p>You’ll study painting, drawing and printmaking as three interconnected disciplines, developing your practice through experimentation with materials, processes, approaches and ideas.</p>
How will the course help me develop my own artistic voice?
<p>The curriculum is designed to nurture your curiosity, proficiency and confidence within a specialist studio environment, giving you the support and practical skills to discover your own artistic voice.</p>
How will I be taught?
<p>You’ll be guided by experienced lecturers and tutors and learn through hands-on experimentation, plus seminars, lectures and group discussions to explore diverse histories and current debates. Regular tutorials support you to synthesise your experiences across painting, drawing and printmaking to craft an ambitious practice shaped by your research and what you care about.</p>
What practical techniques will I learn?
<p>Workshops introduce traditional techniques including intaglio printing and making your own paints from raw pigments, explored alongside technologies and know-how from across the university’s world-class facilities.</p>
What kind of studio environment will I study in?
<p>You’ll study amongst your peers in light and airy studios, with dedicated facilities to support the development of an ambitious practice.</p>
Will I have opportunities to exhibit my work?
<p>Yes. Links with cultural and community organisations in and around Plymouth offer opportunities throughout the course to explore the roles of an artist and to exhibit your work.</p>
Who will I learn from?
<p>You’ll learn from professional artists and experienced lecturers and tutors who understand the challenges and rewards of creative practice and can guide you in developing your own visual language.</p>
What skills will I graduate with?
<p>You’ll develop depth of knowledge and a range of specialist and transferable skills intended to support a fulfilling and enduring creative career.</p>
What career paths can this degree lead to?
<p>Career opportunities include: Exhibiting Artists, Teachers, Lecturers, Art Therapists, Illustrators, Curators, Producers, Gallery Managers, Arts Administrators, Community Arts Organisers and Creative Entrepreneurs.</p>
Can this course support progression to postgraduate study?
<p>Yes. Many award-winning students continue to postgraduate study at Arts University Plymouth and other institutions nationally, such as The Royal Drawing School and The Royal College of Art.</p>
Integrated Foundation Year

This degree is also available as a four-year Integrated Foundation option, with a preparatory first year that will support your creative development.
