Overview

Be part of an exciting, multi-discipline BA (Hons) Fine Art course, set within our dynamic culture of making in our purpose-built studios.

We take great pride in our course to create a thriving fine art community.

Explore current student artwork on our course Instagram: @aubfineart

  • Register your interest
  • Book an Open Day
  • Order our prospectus
  • How to apply

Consider fine art in its widest sense.

On BA (Hons) Fine Art at AUB, you'll have the freedom to test out many different approaches to your artistic practice and to refine your way of working. The course team have learnt that fine art making and problem-solving skills are transferable to wide-ranging career pathways – these range from being an artist, an educator or creative entrepreneurial business opportunities. The course aims to nurture well-rounded, creatively confident individuals who are flexible in their outlook to employment opportunities.

Experiment to find your medium.

Experimental workshops in painting, drawing, printmaking and sculpture provide you with a solid foundation in testing the physical and conceptual potential of materials that you can then develop further.
