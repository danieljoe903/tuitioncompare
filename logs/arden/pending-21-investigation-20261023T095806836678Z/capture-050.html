Course Overview

Launch your career with confidence

Arden University’s FdA Business and Innovation foundation degree equips you with the core knowledge and real-world skills needed to thrive in today’s fast-moving business world. This two-year Level 5 qualification builds a strong base in areas like finance, digital marketing, innovation, and people management—giving you the tools to make a real impact from day one.

Whether you want to move up in your current role, start your own business, or continue on to a full undergraduate degree, this course is designed to help you plan, achieve, and accelerate your next step.

You’ll also benefit from modules designed to boost your academic confidence and career development—ensuring you’re ready to succeed at university and in your career.

Why study this course?

  • Designed to develop entrepreneurial thinking and innovation skills to help you stand out
  • Learn key business functions including digital marketing, project and supply chain management, and people leadership
  • Gain the skills to take on early management roles across a range of industries
  • Dedicated academic skills module helps you succeed, no matter your education background
  • No exams—only practical assessments, with many based on real-world business scenarios
  • Work-based project module to apply what you’ve learned in your workplace or a real organisation

Course details and modules

Build your business skills with a 2-year foundation degree

This FdA course gives you a solid grounding in business fundamentals while exploring how today’s organisations operate. You’ll develop practical management and leadership skills, and gain a well-rounded understanding of the core areas that drive business success.

Flexible study with support

Delivered full-time through blended learning, this course has been carefully structured to help you balance your studies with your other responsibilities. You’ll attend all in-person classes on just one day per week, giving you the flexibility to continue working or managing other priorities alongside your education.

Pathway to a full degree

After completing your FdA, you will be eligible to study the final year (Level 6) of a related undergraduate degree—many of which are accredited by professional bodies such as CMI and ILM—giving you a full BA qualification and boosting your career potential.

The course gave me the confidence and skills to go for senior jobs that I wouldn’t have previously. I have been promoted several times since.

Martin Scahill
BSc (Hons) Business & Computing (Top-Up)

What you’ll learn

You’ll study one core module per study block. Alongside this, you’ll also work on Learning for Life: Skills for Study and Work (Business) in your first year and the Workplace Impact Project - Applied Work-Based Learning for Innovation and Impact in your second year—both of which run across three blocks to support deeper learning and real-world application.

Modules

Level 4 course modules
  • Entrepreneurship and Business Innovation (30 credits)
  • Learning for Life: Skills for Study and Work (Business) (30 credits)
  • Principles of Business (30 credits)
  • Sustainable Business Practice (30 credits)
Level 5 course modules
  • Digital Marketing (30 credits)
  • Managing People and Organisations (30 credits)
  • Project and Supply Chain Management (30 credits)
  • Workplace Impact Project - Applied Work-Based Learning for Innovation and Impact (30 credits)

Study options

This course is available to study through Arden’s blended learning study mode.

Daytime classes are available in London Ealing, London Stratford, London Holborn, Manchester, Birmingham and Leeds

Evening classes are available in London Stratford,, London Holborn, Manchester, and  Birmingham

Friday Evening and Saturdays are another option. You’ll have one class on a Friday evening and one class on Saturday each week. These are available in London Stratford.

Full-time blended learning

How does it work?

Our blended learning model combines the structure of in-person teaching with the flexibility of online learning. If you're studying in the daytime, you’ll attend eight hours of timetabled classes in person one day per week at your chosen Arden campus—designed to free up the rest of your week for work or other responsibilities. Alternatively, if you opt for evening or weekend study modes, you'll attend four hours of in-person classes twice a week, giving you greater flexibility to fit learning around a busy schedule.

You’ll also engage in around 25 hours of independent study each week via iLearn, our virtual learning environment. iLearn gives you access to all the resources you need, including recorded lectures, learning materials, and our Library Portal—an extensive digital library with thousands of journals, eBooks, and academic databases.

Blended learning is the best option for students who prefer the structure of regular face to face classes with their tutor and fellow classmates, and is particularly suited to students who live within commuting or driving distance of one of our UK campuses.

If you're a student from England who is enrolling on a blended learning course with Arden (based in one of our UK campuses) then you can apply for a Tuition Fee Loan and Maintenance Loan to help pay for your living expenses. You can find more information on our fees and funding page. If you're a student from Scotland, Wales or Northern Ireland you can find information about obtaining a student loan to cover your costs from your student finance body.

This course is also available to study full-time at our campus in Berlin, Germany, where you’ll have the opportunity to experience life as an international student in the heart of one of Europe’s most dynamic and historic cities.

Entry requirements

At Arden University we consider applications on a case by case basis. If you have significant work experience, have qualifications you have gained elsewhere, or a degree or qualification that isn't a clear pathway to this degree - we are more than happy to discuss your application.

Entry Requirements

We welcome motivated learners from all backgrounds. Whether you're returning to education or just starting out, we’ll support you every step of the way.

Standard entry:

  • Minimum 60 credits from Level 2/3 qualification (e.g., GCSE, A-Levels, BTECs, Access to HE, or equivalent) with a minimum of 15 credits at level 3)
  • Current professional C.V.

Non-standard entry:

  • 2 years’ work experience in a relevant role (industry specific work experience or professional certificates) of which some must have been completed in the last 12 months.
  • English proficiency - IELTS 6.0 (no less than 5.5 in any element); or TOEFL iBT 80; or equivalent) 

English language proficiency:

  • IELTS 6.0 (no less than 5.5 in any component) or equivalent
  • Previous study in English may also be accepted
