Fees for International students

Home Study full-time, on campus in the UK with Arden University Fees for International students

When planning your studies, it’s important to consider additional costs. You will need to pay a portion of your tuition fees as a deposit once you have accepted your place on your course. You will be advised of the deposit value for your chosen course during your application. This payment will secure your place. You can then pay the remaining amount of your fees in full or in instalments.

Tuition fees:

Our tuition fees are:

Undergraduate top-up: £16,000

Postgraduate: £17,500

With our introductory offer this is reduced to:

Undergraduate top-up: £13,000 in London and £12,000 in Manchester

Postgraduate: £13,500 in London and £12,500 in Manchester.

Find out more on our scholarships page

Terms and conditions apply

Programme-specific fees are available on our fees and funding page.

You can choose to pay for your studies in full or enter a payment plan to pay in instalments for each year of study. This can be arranged with your student recruitment advisor prior to your enrolment.

Accommodation fees:

We advise you begin looking for accommodation in London or Manchester as soon as possible. Although Arden does not offer university accommodation, we have partnered with trusted accommodation providers offering a variety of city-centre student housing options.

Accommodation costs can vary, however private rentals can be in high competition. Learn more about accommodation

Living expenses:

Budget for essentials such as food, transport, and other daily costs. These living expenses also play a role in your visa application.

Visa and immigration fees:

These include the visa application fee and the immigration health surcharge, which grants you access to public healthcare in the UK.
