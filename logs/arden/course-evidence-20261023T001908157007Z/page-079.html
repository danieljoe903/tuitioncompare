Course Overview

The BSc (Hons) Digital Marketing with Foundation Year at Arden University will help you pave the way for a rewarding career in digital marketing. Digital marketing is now vital to every forward-thinking organisation – creating genuine opportunities for ambitious marketers to get themselves noticed.

Our digital marketing degree has been designed for people who want to shape the future of marketing through digital channels. Whether you're an aspiring digital marketer or seeking to enhance your existing knowledge, this course will equip you with the skills you need for a successful career in digital marketing.

What makes studying at Arden University different is our commitment to vocational learning. Our innovative approach focuses on cultivating the technical competencies essential for success in digital marketing. Through a blend of theoretical knowledge, hands-on experience, and an internship programme incorporated into your degree studies in Level 5, you'll develop the practical skills needed to thrive.

In addition to DMI accreditation, we are also working towards securing accreditation from the Chartered Institute of Marketing (CIM), offering further industry recognition and enhanced professional credibility.

Your assessments on this course have been designed to simulate real-world scenarios, ensuring that you can apply your knowledge and skills with confidence. From crafting social media strategies to pitching digital marketing campaigns, every assignment prepares you for the challenges of the industry.

Our bachelor’s in digital marketing isn't just about acquiring knowledge – it's about embracing diversity and fostering global perspectives. With case studies from around the world and mentorship from industry experts, you'll gain insights into how digital marketing is practised across different cultures and contexts.

Unlock your potential as a digital marketing trailblazer. With our supportive learning environment and industry-aligned curriculum, the possibilities are endless.

Course details and modules

Arden's foundation year modules provide a personalised introduction to university study, equipping you with academic skills and subject-specific knowledge vital for degree success. Beginning with business fundamentals, the programme progresses to impart the technical competencies needed for a digital marketing career. It emphasises high-demand industry subjects like social media management, SEO, CX/UX design, content management, digital communications, brand management, and data analytics.

Digital Resource Hub

The Digital Resource Hub offers practical certifications to enhance your digital marketing studies. You’ll have access to free resources in web analytics, SEO, email marketing, social media marketing, digital advertising, website creation, copywriting, and graphic design from leading organisations such as Google, HubSpot, LinkedIn Learning, Adobe, and more. Additionally, the Hub features the latest podcasts, videos, and articles in digital marketing, making it a valuable resource for staying updated on industry trends

How do we assess you?

Our practical and innovative assignments are specifically designed to enhance your employability. That means you’ll be given the opportunity to apply your knowledge and digital skills to 'real-world' scenarios. Examples include:

  • Creating a social media strategy
  • Building a crisis PR plan for an organisational case study
  • Pitching a digital marketing campaign
  • Mobile app prototyping and creative portfolio development

Assessment methods range from traditional reports, presentations, case studies, and projects to more digital skills-based formats like websites, reflective blogs, vodcasts, creative portfolios, pitches, and CX/UX journeys.

Full-time blended learning

How does it work?

Our blended learning degree combines interactive, face-to-face campus teaching with flexible online study via iLearn, our 24/7 digital campus.

  • Campus schedule: Attend 8 hours of timetabled classes each week at your chosen UK campus. You can spread your classes over 2 days per week, or choose our new 1-day-per-week option if you are studying a Foundation Year degree course.
  • Independent study: Complement your campus classes with approximately 32 hours per week of self-guided study, module activities, and assessment prep on iLearn.
  • Free study materials: Access our digital Library Portal featuring thousands of e-books, journals, and databases, which are all provided completely free of charge, saving you the cost of expensive textbooks.

Blended learning is ideal for students who want the structure of regular face-to-face classes alongside the flexibility to fit full-time study around work, family, and personal commitments.

Fees and funding

Because blended learning is a full-time study mode, you remain eligible for full UK student finance:

Students in England: You can apply for both a Tuition Fee Loan and a Maintenance Loan to help cover your living costs.

  • Students in Scotland, Wales, & Northern Ireland: Funding is available through your regional student finance body.

For full details on eligibility and how to apply, visit our Fees and Funding page.

Entry requirements

At Arden University we consider applications on a case by case basis. If you have significant work experience, have qualifications you have gained elsewhere, or a degree or qualification that isn't a clear pathway to this degree - we are more than happy to discuss your application.

Eligibility

To be eligible for our BSc (Hons) Digital Marketing, you should normally have:

  • A minimum of 45 credits at Level 3 completed within the last 3 years, or an equivalent qualification.
  • Applicants may be considered based on a minimum of 2 years  of professional work experience in a business or marketing-related field, evidenced by a CV and reference if they do not meet the qualification requirements.

English proficiency

  • IELTS 6.0 (no less than 5.5 in any element); or TOEFL iBT 80; or equivalent
  • Alternatively, evidence you have previously studied in English, such as a letter to show that the medium of Instruction was in English, can be accepted.

What will the BSc (Hons) Digital Marketing do for me?

This degree has been designed to provide you with the practical skills to meet the growing demands of the digital marketing sector. We are in the process of implementing industry micro-credentials and live internships provided by industry leaders and organisations to nurture your digital skills. While studying, you will:

  • Cultivate essential ‘life skills’, such as self-awareness and management, digital literacy, research, argumentation, numeracy, critical thinking, and analytical ability. 
  • Demonstrate proficiency in some of the most important skills in digital marketing today.
  • Gain insight into applying your digital marketing expertise within a professional setting.
  • Acquire the skills necessary to transition into a digital marketing role or advance to a higher position within the marketing field.

What can I do after Arden University?

The main objective of this programme is to support your practical implementation of modern digital marketing practices. This ensures you graduate with the necessary skills and knowledge to succeed in the industry. Here are a few career options you might consider:

  • Digital Marketing Analyst
  • Digital Marketing Manager
  • Social Media Manager
  • Content Strategist
  • Copywriter
  • UX/CX Specialist
  • SEO Specialist
  • Email Marketing Specialist
  • PPC Manager

Industry skills

Digital Marketing Institute (DMI)

We are in the process of achieving accreditation from the DMI.

Highly acclaimed and globally recognised across the digital marketing sector, the Digital Marketing Institute (DMI) is the global standard in digital marketing certification and works to support today’s marketers in achieving their full professional potential.

The DMI partners with representatives from leading brands such as Google, Facebook, Microsoft, and LinkedIn to build a framework that bridges the digital skills gap, ensuring your curriculum is up-to-date and meets the real employability needs of the digital marketing industry.

While you’re studying your DMI accredited course with us, we’ll cover the cost of your student membership, giving you access to a wide range of valuable resources, including exclusive industry-focused short courses, webinars, articles, and toolkits, careers guidance, and networking opportunities with its 170,000+ global members.

Chartered Institute of Marketing (CIM)

We are in the process of achieving accreditation from the CIM.

Students completing this course will gain exemptions for the CIM Certificate and Diploma qualifications, meaning you will only need to complete one or two modules instead of three.

The Chartered Institute of Marketing (CIM) is the world’s largest organisation for professional marketers and plays a key role in setting industry standards.

The CIM helps and supports its members at every stage of their marketing careers. It focuses on developing your marketing capabilities and informing you about industry developments and employment opportunities across the sector – providing you with the ‘4Cs’ of CIM membership: Career, Capability, Community and Content.

The cost of your CIM Affiliate Membership is included while you study with us, giving you access to all these resources and more.

Association to Advance Collegiate Schools of Business (AACSB) membership

Arden University is a member of the prestigious Association to Advance Collegiate Schools of Business (AACSB). AACSB International is a global association of leaders in education and business dedicated to supporting and advancing quality business education worldwide.

This membership reflects the fact that Arden University has successfully matched the high standards required by the AACSB and enables the sharing of ideas and the exchange of best practices between global business schools.

Membership also gives our faculty members access to in-depth data and research to support the development of our courses and teaching methods, ensuring that we are able to provide you with the best possible university experience.

Business Graduates Association (BGA)

The BGA is a membership and quality assurance body. As a member, Arden University have signed up to the BGA Charter, committing their institution to responsible management practices and lifelong learning, positively impacting their students, communities, and the economy. This shows our students that we are demonstrating responsible management ourselves, as well as it being a central theme that runs through our own courses.

As Arden University is a member of the Business Graduates Association (BGA), students will receive complimentary BGA student and graduate membership. Through this membership, current students will have access to the BGA Career Development Centre, packed with interactive tools, e-learning content, partner discounts and the latest job opportunities. This is a lifetime access, which means students will continue to have access to these services even after they graduate.
