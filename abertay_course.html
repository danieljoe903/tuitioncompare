Why study Accounting and Finance?

Build your career in accounting and finance at Abertay University. Our BSc (Hons) Accounting and Finance combines rigorous academic foundations with hands on, practical experience, preparing you not just for your first job, but for a rewarding long-term career in finance.

We've applied for accreditation from four leading professional bodies - CIMA, ACCA, AIA and ICAS. This means after successfully graduating, you’ll qualify for exemptions from some professional exams, in order to speed up your route to professional qualifications.

Develop key financial and business skills

Your first year provides a broad business foundation, where you’ll explore different areas before focusing on what interests you most. You'll build solid knowledge of accounting, finance, economics and business operations.

Your first year is shared with our Business Management and Marketing programmes. As you progress, you'll tackle more specialised areas, including corporate finance, international financial accounting, management accounting, taxation and auditing.

Throughout, you'll develop the professional judgement and problem solving skills that employers look for. You’ll learn to address complex challenges with creativity, inclusiveness and sound professional judgement.

Careers: what can this Accounting and Finance degree lead to?

The skills you’ll develop are in demand, offering strong career prospects and earning potential. While many graduates pursue professional accountancy qualifications, this degree opens doors to a range of roles, including:

  • Traditional accountancy routes: Chartered Accountant, Certified Accountant, Auditor, Tax Specialist.

  • Financial management: Finance Manager, Financial Analyst, Credit Controller, Risk Manager.

  • Specialist financial roles: FP&A Analyst, Business Analyst, Management Consultant, Stockbroker.

Our graduates typically progress quickly, bringing strong numeracy and analytical skills that employers value.

Real-world experience through industry connections

We know you're thinking about what comes after graduation. So are we. You’ll connect with the world of work via expert guest speakers from industry, real-world case studies, projects covering genuine business challenges and visits to local/national organisations. You'll also have opportunities to study abroad at one of our international partner institutions, or go on a work placement and get experience in a professional setting.

We arrange for you to meet representatives of the major professional accounting bodies every year, so you can plan your career pathway and understand the qualifications that will take you furthest. We also have industry speakers and projects from a variety of sectors including organisations such as: 

  • Dundee Social Enterprise Network 

  • Stagecoach 

  • Harris Tweed 

  • Dundee Football Club 

This all helps you build a CV that stands out to employers, and begin to make connections that could shape your career.

Graduate with key financial management skills

You'll graduate with a thorough understanding of how organisations operate within their economic, legal and global contexts. 

Whether you see yourself working for a multinational corporation, a local firm, a public sector organisation, you'll have the financial expertise and professional skills to succeed.

If entrepreneurship is your thing, our on-campus business incubator, Bell Street Ventures, provides all the support you need to help you turn your ideas into reality.

What financial skills will you learn?

Get prepared for a fast moving career in accounting, finance and business. You will:

  • Build up your integrated business, accounting and finance expertise, and become fluent in data literacy.

  • Interpret and present financial/numerical data to a high, professional standard.

  • Develop analytical, problem solving and decision-making capabilities.

  • Operate with integrity, accountability, and respect for ethical principles.

  • Learn to communicate effectively across different kinds of audiences.

  • Cultivate an inclusive approach to managing people, systems and organisations.

  • Learn to work both independently and collaborate in teams.

How the course works

Your four-year learning journey

Year 1: Build your business foundation

Explore accounting, finance, economics, marketing, and management. This year is shared with our Business Management and Marketing degrees, giving you a broad introduction to business before you specialise in year two.

Year 2: Deepen your financial expertise

Specialise in financial and management accounting, economics, and business analytics. Professional skills modules help you develop your CV, your professional presence, and the judgement employers look for.

Year 3: Apply your knowledge

Tackle specialised areas including corporate finance, taxation, and auditing. Take a work placement, study abroad, or work on real business challenges through consultancy projects and competitions.

Year 4: Your capstone project

A substantial final project where you demonstrate the depth and rigour of your financial expertise, supported by academics and industry mentors.

Learning and assessment

We use varied assessment methods to help you develop different professional skills:

  • Portfolio projects to build evidence of your work for future employers.

  • Presentations to develop confidence when communicating complex information.

  • Examinations to prepare you for professional qualification exams.

  • Group work to experience collaborative problem solving.

  • Simulations to help apply your learning in realistic scenarios.

  • Work assessments if you undertake a placement.

We also have international partner institutions, from the US to Asia, and all stops in between, where you can study in English for a term or a year. This is an excellent opportunity to boost your CV, as it shows your willingness to experience different cultures and methods of working. 

Delivered via blended learning

This degree uses blended learning, combining face-to-face teaching with online elements.

In the early stages, most teaching happens on campus, with the online components increasing as you progress and develop confidence with digital learning tools. This approach mirrors the modern workplace, where remote collaboration and online learning are increasingly common. You'll graduate comfortable with the digital tools that are increasingly standard in professional life.

 

Accreditations (applied for)

We have applied for this degree to be accredited by CIMA, ACCA, AIA and ICAS.

Accreditations are a mark of the high calibre of the skills the degree delivers. They are industry standards for quality, and mean that the content, structure, and method of delivery have been assessed and approved by experts in that field or sector.

In this case, accreditation means you may also be entitled to exemptions from some professional exams, meaning you’ll qualify faster. Visit the relevant websites to find out more.

Not sure if you’re eligible for entry?

If you have the potential and motivation to study at university, regardless of your background or personal circumstances, we welcome your application.

We understand some people have faced extra challenges before applying to university, which is why we consider the background in which your academic grades have been achieved when making an offer.

If you expect to receive passes in three Scottish Highers (grades A–C) and have…

  • been in care, or are a young carer yourself
  • attended a school or lived in an area where not many people go to university
  • are eligible for free school meals
  • are a young person estranged from your family
  • are a government‑recognised refugee or have asylum seeker status
  • are a registered pupil with sustained engagement in a targeted aspiration‑raising programme such as LIFT OFF, LEAPS, FOCUS West or Aspire North

…we encourage you to submit an application.

US High School Diploma

Entry to Year 1

Applicants studying a US High School Diploma will be required to pass with a GPA of 2.8 (on a 4-point scale). Any essential subjects to be achieved with minimum grade B.

If applying for year 1 entry, standardised testing is optional, however may make an application more competitive, particularly if the High School Diploma score is lower than stated above, or if the essential subject is not covered in the curriculum.

We will consider a lower High School Diploma score if sufficient evidence exists to support an offer such as strong scores in standardised tests, professional experience in the subject area, extracurricular activities linked to the programme, college classes, etc.

Standardised tests accepted for Y1 entry may include one or more of the following:

  • SAT: Minimum overall score of 1050, with a minimum score of 500 in each component.
  • ACT: Minimum overall score of 24, with a minimum score of 24 in each component.
  • AP: Relevant AP tests at grade 3 or above (taken in the last 3 years)

AP tests are an ideal way to meet essential subject pre-requisites. If no testing is available, strong grades in year 11 of the US High School Diploma in required subjects will be considered.

Entry to Year 2

Applicants studying a US High School Diploma will be required to pass with a GPA of 3.3 (on a 4-point scale). Any essential subjects to be achieved with minimum grade A.

If applying for year 2 entry, standardised testing is required.

Standardised tests accepted for Y2 entry may include one or more of the following:

  • SAT: Minimum overall score of 1200, with a minimum score of 550 in each component.
  • ACT: Minimum overall score of 26, with a minimum score of 24 in each component.
  • AP: 2 relevant AP tests at grade 4 or above (taken in the last 3 years)

If applying through UCAS, please send your transcript and any test scores directly to admissions@abertay.ac.uk, quoting your UCAS number and full name.

If applying directly, you will be given the opportunity to upload your transcript and test scores within your application.

As well as Abertay scholarships for English, Welsh, Northern Irish and international students, there are a range of corporate and philanthropic scholarships available. Some are course specific, many are not. There are some listed below or you can visit the Undergraduate scholarship pages.

Scholarships

Abertay RUK Scholarship: Business

A scholarship for prospective undergraduate Business students applying from England, Wales or Northern Ireland.

Abertay International Scholarship

An award of up to £3,000 per year for up to a maximum of four years, for prospective new international undergraduate and postgraduate taught students.

The Robert Reid Bursary

Two £1,000 awards for students who have overcome challenges to attend university.

ICAS Foundation Bursary

Bursaries of up to £2,500 per year from The ICAS Foundation, for both current and those planning to be, accounting/finance students.

Scotland's first Adobe Creative Campus

Abertay is the first institution in Scotland to be an Adobe Creative Campus. Through our partnership, all Abertay students have access to Adobe Express, empowering them to enhance their digital skills with industry-leading tools.

The Adobe Creative Campus programme is dedicated to empowering staff and students across all disciplines with essential digital skills for success in both the workplace and the classroom. The initiative involves a select group of colleges and universities.

The Discover Uni dataset

The Discover Uni dataset (formerly Unistats) is an official source of information about higher education. It collates comparable information in areas students have identified as important in making decisions about what and where to study.

Support for Ukrainian students

We're keen to offer help to Ukrainian students who may wish to transfer from their existing institution in Ukraine or to register with us as new students for intake in September. There will be no tuition fees charged for the duration of the degree programme, as those with refugee status are treated as ‘Home/Scottish’ students and will also have access to the Student Awards Agency for Scotland bursary and student loans. Our Recruitment Team can help guide applicants.
